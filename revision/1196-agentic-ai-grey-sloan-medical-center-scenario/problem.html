Agentic AI Fundamentals › 2. Applications and the Healthcare Scenario › Grey Sloan Medical Center scenario

Challenges: high diagnostic error rates, long patient wait times, and inefficient patient data management. The solution was a multi-agent deployment with three groups: diagnostic support agents, patient management agents and administrative agents.

Agent typeTasksResults
Diagnostic supportAnalyze radiology images, lab results and patient histories; give real-time diagnostic recommendationsFewer diagnostic errors, more accurate complex evaluations
Patient managementAutomate scheduling around provider availability and patient needs, optimized with real-time data30% more patients seen, fewer cancellations, shorter waits
AdministrativeAutomate data entry, update patient records, handle billing and claimsFewer errors, lower administrative workload
