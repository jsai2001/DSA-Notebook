Q. What is the difference between Continuous Integration, Continuous Delivery, and Continuous Deployment?

  • CI: developers merge frequently; each change is automatically built and tested.
  • Continuous Delivery: every passing build is automatically prepared and deployable, but a human approves the release to production.
  • Continuous Deployment: every passing change is released to production automatically, with no manual gate.
