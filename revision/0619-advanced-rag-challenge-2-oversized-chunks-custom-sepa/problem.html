Advanced RAG › 7. Challenges: Citing Sources, Fixing Chunk Size › Challenge 2: oversized chunks (custom separator)

CharacterTextSplitter defaults: separator="\n\n", is_separator_regex=False. If the text has no double newline inside the size window, it can't split, so chunks exceed the size and warnings appear. Fix: use "\n".

The separator is where the splitter may cut: "\n\n" waits for paragraph breaks, "\n" can cut at any line break, giving correctly sized chunks while keeping coherence at line boundaries.
