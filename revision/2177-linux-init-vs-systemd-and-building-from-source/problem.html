Linux › 5. Advanced Linux Topics › init vs. systemd and building from source

Q. init vs. systemd? init is sequential and script-based. systemd is parallel and dependency-driven, with logging and cgroups.

Q. How do you compile and install from source? Extract (tar -xzvf), then ./configure, make, sudo make install.
