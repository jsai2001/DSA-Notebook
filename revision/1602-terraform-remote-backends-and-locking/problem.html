Terraform › 5. State Management › Remote backends and locking

Q. How do you configure a remote backend (S3, Terraform Cloud)?

Q. What are the benefits of remote state? Collaboration, locking, safety and versioning.

Q. Can you lock the state file, and why? Locking is automatic with remote backends (for example DynamoDB with S3). It prevents race conditions.

Q. How do you share state across a team? Use a remote backend with access control and locking.

Q. What happens with two simultaneous terraform apply runs? With local state they overwrite each other, which is chaos. With a remote backend and locking, one run fails.
