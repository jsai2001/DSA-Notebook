Notebook 03_02_clustering.ipynb. Repeatedly assigns points to the nearest center, then moves each center. Scale features first so none dominates.

Clusters tend to match species although the model never saw the labels.

Choosing K, the elbow method. Inertia measures how tight points sit around their center. It always falls as K grows, so pick the elbow where the drop flattens. Domain knowledge matters too.
