Kubernetes › 2. Architecture › What each component does

Q. Explain the role of the kube-apiserver. The central hub. It exposes the API, handles requests, authenticates and authorizes them, and updates etcd.

Q. What does the kube-scheduler do? It places Pods on nodes based on resources, policies, and constraints.

Q. What is the purpose of the kube-controller-manager? It runs controllers that keep the cluster state (for example replicas and nodes) as desired.

Q. What is etcd, and why is it important? A distributed store for cluster state. It is critical for consistency and control plane operations.

Q. How does the kubelet work on a worker node? It is the node agent. It starts and stops containers, ensures Pods run, and reports to the control plane.

Q. What is the role of kube-proxy? It manages networking. It sets up rules for Service IPs and load balances traffic to Pods, using iptables or IPVS.

Q. What is the Cloud Controller Manager, and when is it used? It links Kubernetes to cloud APIs for nodes, load balancers, and storage. It is used in cloud-hosted clusters.

Q. How does Kubernetes ensure high availability of the control plane? Multiple master nodes, replicated etcd, leader election, and load-balanced API servers.
