StatefulSet, DaemonSet, Job and CronJob Kubernetes

Kubernetes › 3. Pods and Workloads › StatefulSet, DaemonSet, Job and CronJob

Q. What is a StatefulSet, and how does it differ from a Deployment? It manages stateful apps with stable names and storage. Unlike a Deployment, it is ordered and persistent.

Q. What is a DaemonSet, and when is it useful? It runs one Pod per node, which suits monitoring, logging, or network agents.

Q. What is a Job and a CronJob?

  • Job: a one-time task that runs to completion.
  • CronJob: scheduled Jobs (for example backups).
