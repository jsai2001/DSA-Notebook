Debugging failures Kubernetes

Kubernetes › 10. Monitoring and Troubleshooting › Debugging failures

Q. How do you debug a Pod that is crashing? Check logs (kubectl logs --previous), events, and the Pod spec.

Q. What are common reasons for a Pod to be in CrashLoopBackOff? Application errors, a bad image, resource limits, or probe failures.

Q. How do you troubleshoot a service that is not responding? Check endpoints, Pods, and networking, and test with kubectl port-forward.
