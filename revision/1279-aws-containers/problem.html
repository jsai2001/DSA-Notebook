Q. ECS vs. EKS?

  • ECS: AWS-native container orchestration. Simple.
  • EKS: managed Kubernetes. Flexible and portable.

Q. What is AWS Fargate? Serverless containers for ECS and EKS. There are no EC2 instances to manage; you only define resources.
