6. TensorFlow and MNIST on Vertex AI GCP for Machine Learning

GCP for Machine Learning › 6. TensorFlow and MNIST on Vertex AI

TensorFlow: open-source library from Google Brain (used by Google, Airbnb, Uber, Netflix) for image recognition, NLP, speech, predictive modeling. On GCP it integrates deeply with Vertex AI, so prefer it for custom models where possible.

IntegrationDescription
Pre-installed in NotebooksReady in notebook instances
Custom TrainingSubmit TF jobs to Vertex AI Training
Pre-built ContainersGoogle TF serving containers for deployment
Distributed TrainingScale across GPUs/TPUs
Model Registry / EndpointsStore TF models, serve online predictions

MNIST is the "Hello World" of ML: 70,000 grayscale images of handwritten digits 0–9, 28×28 pixels, an image-classification benchmark.

The tensorflow.org tutorials have beginner and expert MNIST versions; work through both before custom models on Vertex AI.
