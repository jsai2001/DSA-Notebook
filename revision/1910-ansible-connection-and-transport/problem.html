Connection and transport Ansible

Ansible › 1. Ansible Fundamentals › Connection and transport

Q. How does Ansible connect to remote hosts? Via SSH by default. The control node authenticates (keys or passwords), sends modules, runs them with Python, and removes them. WinRM is used for Windows.

Q. Default transport mechanism? SSH. It is secure, agentless, and relies on the existing SSH setup on the hosts.
