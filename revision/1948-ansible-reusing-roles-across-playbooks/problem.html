Ansible › 8. Roles and Galaxy › Reusing roles across playbooks

Q. Reuse roles across playbooks? Store them in roles/ and call them with roles: or include_role.

roles: applies the role at play level, before the play's tasks. include_role applies it as a task, giving control over when it runs (inside a loop or conditionally).
