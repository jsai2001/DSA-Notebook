Challenge: which dog is most similar to the cats? Advanced RAG

Advanced RAG › 9. Image Search Demos › Challenge: which dog is most similar to the cats?

Approach: for each dog image, retrieve top-4; count cat images, weighted by rank (rank i gets 4 - i points: 4, 3, 2, 1). A cat at rank 0 and another at rank 2 scores 4 + 2 = 6.
