LangGraph › 2. Concepts: Chatbots, Agentic AI, State vs Memory, LangGraph › 2.1 Conversational AI and chatbots

  • Conversational AI simulates human conversation with natural language understanding, processing and generation. A chatbot is built on it to infer intent and respond.
  • Chatbot traits: free-form text in and out, multi-language, text/voice/multimedia, a persona and purpose, backend integration, conversational memory, small-talk handling.
