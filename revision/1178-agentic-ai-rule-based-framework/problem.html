Agentic AI Fundamentals › 6. Policy Frameworks › Rule-based framework

  • Predefined rules: specific rules the system must follow, such as never attempting to injure a human.
  • Adaptability: behavior can change with conditions while still obeying the rules.
  • Compliance: ensures predictable behavior.

Example: alter the material mix in a temperature-sensitive manufacturing process as the weather changes, while still following every safety rule.
