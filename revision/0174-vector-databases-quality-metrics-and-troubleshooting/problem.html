Quality metrics and troubleshooting Vector Databases

Vector Databases › 10. Use case 2: RAG concepts › Quality metrics and troubleshooting

  • Knowledge base: coverage, freshness, accuracy. Retrieval: precision, recall, latency. Answer: accuracy, relevance, completeness, user satisfaction.
  • Poor answers: was the right context retrieved? chunk size? prompt instructions? Top-K?
  • Slow: tune nprobe, reduce Top-K, cache frequent queries (see section 9), faster embedding model.
  • Irrelevant results: better chunking, scalar filters, cleaner curation, different embedding model.
