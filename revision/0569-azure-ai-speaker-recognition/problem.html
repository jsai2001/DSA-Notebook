Azure AI Services › 3. Speech › Speaker Recognition

  • Recognizes who is speaking, even a voice the system never heard before. Uses: identification (who is it?) and verification (speak to gain access).
  • Flow: create an identification profile per speaker (30+ seconds of audio each), then submit a new sample (not used to build profiles); the service returns which profiled speaker matches.
