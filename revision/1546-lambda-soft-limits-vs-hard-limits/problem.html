AWS Lambda › 7. Concurrency, scaling and limits › Soft limits vs hard limits

Q67. What are soft limits vs hard limits?

  • Soft limits (quotas): adjustable through Service Quotas, for example the default 1,000 concurrency and function or layer storage.
  • Hard limits: fixed by the platform, for example the 15-min timeout, 6 MB sync and 256 KB async payload, 10 GB memory, 10 GB /tmp, 250 MB unzipped zip and 512 KB total env var size. Design around hard limits and request increases for soft ones.
