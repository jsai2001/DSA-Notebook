Azure Advisor AZ-900

AZ-900 › 12. Monitoring tools › Azure Advisor

Helps keep resources highly available and efficient, and can sometimes fix problems automatically.

  • Advisor score (0 to 100%) shows how well you follow best practices, by category, with a monthly history.
  • Categories: Security, Reliability, Operational Excellence, Performance, Cost.
  • Remediation: automated (Fix It button, for example installing a missing agent) or manual (step-by-step instructions).
  • Shows affected resources per recommendation.
