Agentic AI Fundamentals › 1. What Agentic AI Is › Agentic AI and agents

Agentic AI, also called autonomous AI, is changing businesses and organizations worldwide. An agent is a program built to perform specific tasks autonomously. It makes decisions from predefined rules or learned patterns and interacts with users or its environment to achieve goals.

The course path runs from agent fundamentals, to internal agent architectures, to machine learning concepts, to practical applications (robotics, healthcare, transportation), to ethics and societal impact. It targets developers who are starting to implement agentic AI.
