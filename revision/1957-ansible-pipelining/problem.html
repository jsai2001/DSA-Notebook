Pipelining Ansible

Ansible › 9. Advanced Features and Scaling › Pipelining

Pipelining reduces SSH overhead by avoiding a new connection for each task. It needs ControlPersist in SSH and may not work if requiretty is enabled on the targets (disable it in /etc/sudoers).
