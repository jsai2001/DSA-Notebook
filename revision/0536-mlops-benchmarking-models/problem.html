Benchmarking models MLOps

MLOps › 6. Model Management › Benchmarking models

Compares a model and its versions against baselines and competing models after it passes its fitness test and before integration. It runs in an environment closer to production, measuring both performance and operational metrics. A model that looks good in a notebook may need more resources or add latency here.

Setup componentDetail
HardwareProduction-like CPU, memory, disk
Isolated environmentSoftware and network isolated from other activity
Test harnessSame test dataset and expected results for baseline and new model
Test executorsRun cases with required pacing and capacity
Tracking platformLog predictions, latency, errors, resource use; compute metrics
ModeBoth models simultaneously, or one model vs a stored baseline

Metrics: performance (accuracy, F1, precision, recall) and operational (latency, scaling, resource utilization).

Best practices: apples-to-apples conditions, log everything at the finest granularity, automate and integrate into pipelines, use experiment tracking tools for long-term analytics.
