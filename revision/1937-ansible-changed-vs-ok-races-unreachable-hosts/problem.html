Ansible › 11. Tricky and Conceptual Questions › changed vs ok, races, unreachable hosts

Q. Difference between changed and ok? changed means the system was modified. ok means no change was needed.

Q. Handle race conditions? Execution is sequential per host; use serial or run_once for shared resources.

Q. What happens with an unreachable host in the inventory? It is marked UNREACHABLE and the playbook continues, unless any_errors_fatal is set.
