#CharacteristicMeaningExample
1Independent decision-makingOperates with minimal human oversight and divides complex workflows into partsA security camera calls the police when it detects a crime and requests firmware updates on its own
2Goal-orientedSets its own goals and works to achieve themThe camera sets the goal of fighting crime, detects a crime, reports it
3Learning and evolutionLearns from interactions, adapts to new data, improves through machine learningNotices weather affects sales and adjusts orders, such as ordering more hardeners when humidity will be high
4Contextual understandingUnderstands nuance and mimics human reasoningConsiders weather, time of year and market conditions relevant to a customer's problem
5Multi-domain utilityFinds and uses applications and data across domains and platformsCustomer service, healthcare, retail, manufacturing, cybersecurity
