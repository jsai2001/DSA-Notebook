SNS notifications not delivered AWS CloudWatch

AWS CloudWatch › 17. Troubleshooting Alarms and Notifications › SNS notifications not delivered

Q. SNS notifications do not reach recipients. What do you check? The topic subscription, IAM permissions and endpoint validity.

Console: SNS > Topics > MyTopic > Subscriptions to confirm the endpoint is confirmed, check the topic ARN on the alarm, and publish a test message from the SNS console.
