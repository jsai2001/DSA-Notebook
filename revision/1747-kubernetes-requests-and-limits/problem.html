Kubernetes › 7. Scheduling and Resources › Requests and limits

Q. What are resource requests and limits?

  • Requests: the minimum needed.
  • Limits: the maximum allowed (CPU/memory).

Q. What happens when a Pod exceeds its resource limits? CPU is throttled and memory causes an OOM kill.

Q. What is a Quality of Service (QoS) class, and how is it assigned?

  • Guaranteed: limits = requests.
  • Burstable: requests < limits.
  • BestEffort: none set. Assigned automatically.
