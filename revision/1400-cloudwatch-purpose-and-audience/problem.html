AWS CloudWatch › 1. What CloudWatch Is › Purpose and audience

CloudWatch is the monitoring and observability service for AWS resources, applications and services. It serves DevOps engineers, developers, IT managers and security specialists. The course teaches how to derive insights and take actions from metrics, use canaries to watch website and API health, and use X-Ray and RUM for end-to-end tracing and real-user monitoring.
