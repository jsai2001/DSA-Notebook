MLOps › 7. Continuous Integration › Integration patterns

ML and non-ML parts evolve simultaneously. Example: ML side Model v2.0 → v3.0 while non-ML side goes v4.0 → v5.0.

Option 1: Hold ML constantOption 2: Adapt new ML ASAP
ApproachUse ML Service v2.0 through all of non-ML v5.0 development and release; introduce v3.0 in the next releaseUse v2.0 until v3.0 exists, then swap mid-cycle
StabilityStable developmentNew version mid-cycle may cause problems
Time to marketSlower (two separate releases)Faster overall
Issue detectionIssues found laterFound earlier
CollaborationSimplerML and non-ML teams must coordinate closely
AutomationStraightforwardHard: auto-pulling new versions may break the pipeline

Choose by requirements and mitigate the weaknesses with process.
