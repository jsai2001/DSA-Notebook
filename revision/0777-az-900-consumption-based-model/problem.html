Consumption-based model AZ-900

AZ-900 › 2. Cloud concepts: models and shared responsibility › Consumption-based model

You pay for what is allocated to you, not what you actively use. A running idle VM is still billed.

Two cost-control rules:

  1. Do not allocate more than you need (do not provision 8 cores when 2 will do).
  2. Fully use what you allocate (do not leave resources idle).

Auto-scaling, cost alerts and recommendations help with both (Sections 9 and 12).
