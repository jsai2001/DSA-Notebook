Async failures, retries and dead letter handling AWS Lambda

AWS Lambda › 4. Invocation models and event sources › Async failures, retries and dead letter handling

Q32. What happens when an asynchronous invocation fails? Lambda retries automatically twice (3 attempts in total) with delays. If it still fails, the event goes to the configured Dead Letter Queue (SQS or SNS) or On-failure Destination. Tune MaximumRetryAttempts (0 to 2) and MaximumEventAge.

Q36. DLQ vs Lambda Destinations? A DLQ (SQS or SNS) captures only the failed event payload after async retries run out. Destinations route on success or failure and send full context (request plus response or error) to SQS, SNS, EventBridge or another Lambda. They are the modern, more informative option for async invocations.

Q37. When use Destinations over SQS/SNS DLQs? Prefer Destinations when you want success routing, full invocation context (not just the payload) or workflow chaining. Use a plain DLQ when you only need failed payloads for later reprocessing and want the simplest setup. A DLQ applies to async invocations and to ESM sources. Destinations apply to async invocations and to stream ESMs.
