9. TorchAudio PyTorch Hands-On

PyTorch Hands-On › 9. TorchAudio

Mirrors TorchVision: datasets (LibriSpeech, Common Voice, GTZAN music genre, Speech Commands, YESNO), io (load, save, info; WAV/FLAC/MP3; native or SoX backend), transforms as nn.Modules (MelSpectrogram, MFCC, Resample, Spectrogram, TimeStretch, FrequencyMasking/TimeMasking for augmentation), functional (stateless: compute_deltas, phase_vocoder, resample, spectrogram, apply_codec), pipelines (ready-made ASR with Wav2Vec2, HuBERT, Conformer; speaker verification/classification; bundles feature extractor + model + decoder + weights + sample rate + labels), models (Wav2Vec2Model, Wav2Vec2ForCTC, HuBERT, Conformer, Tacotron2 and WaveGlow for synthesis), utils (set_audio_backend, get_audio_backend).
