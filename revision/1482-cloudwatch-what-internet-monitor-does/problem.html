What Internet Monitor does AWS CloudWatch

AWS CloudWatch › 15. Internet Monitor › What Internet Monitor does

Internet issues (slow or interrupted connectivity) hurt user experience, and traditional diagnosis can take days. CloudWatch Internet Monitor uses AWS global networking data to set a baseline for internet-facing traffic performance and availability, gives a global view of internet health, and resolves issues in minutes. It adds worldwide traffic and performance data to CloudWatch, produces alerts with global context, and tells you whether an issue is on your side or the customer side with recommended actions. Think of it as a speedometer and early warning system for a site or app.
