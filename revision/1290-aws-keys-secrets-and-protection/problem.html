AWS › 6. Security and Identity › Keys, secrets and protection

Q. What is AWS KMS? It manages encryption keys and encrypts data in S3, RDS and more.

Q. How do you rotate keys in AWS? KMS rotates yearly (automatic) or you rotate manually: create a new key and update apps.

Q. AWS Secrets Manager vs. KMS?

  • Secrets Manager: stores and rotates secrets.
  • KMS: manages keys.

Q. What is AWS Shield? DDoS protection. Standard is free; Advanced is paid and more robust.

Q. AWS WAF vs. a traditional firewall?

  • WAF: Layer 7 (HTTP) filtering.
  • Traditional: network-level control.

Q. How do you audit and monitor security? CloudTrail (API logs), CloudWatch (alerts) and Config (compliance).
