Experience and limitations Terraform

Terraform › 12. Open-Ended Questions › Experience and limitations

Q. How do you stay updated with Terraform? Docs, GitHub, blogs and labs.

Q. What is the most complex infrastructure you have deployed? A multi-region HA app, with challenges around drift, dependencies and secrets.

Q. How do you explain Terraform to a newbie? "Blueprints for cloud, auto-built."

Q. What are Terraform's limitations? State fragility and no real-time view. Mitigate with remote state and drift checks.

Q. How does Terraform compare with other IaC tools? It is multi-cloud and declarative, versus Ansible (configuration management) and CloudFormation (AWS-only).
