Docker › 2. Docker Images and Dockerfiles › Dockerfile instructions

Q. What are the common Dockerfile instructions? FROM, RUN, CMD, ENTRYPOINT, COPY, ADD, WORKDIR, EXPOSE, ENV.

Q. CMD vs ENTRYPOINT?

  • CMD: default arguments, overridable.
  • ENTRYPOINT: the fixed executable.

Q. What is the purpose of .dockerignore? It excludes files from the build context. Builds are faster and cleaner.
