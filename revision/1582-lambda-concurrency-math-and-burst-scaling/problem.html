Concurrency math and burst scaling AWS Lambda

AWS Lambda › 7. Concurrency, scaling and limits › Concurrency math and burst scaling

Q63. How is total concurrency computed? Concurrency = requests per second x average duration in seconds. For example 100 req/s x 0.2 s = 20 concurrent executions. This (Little's Law) tells you how much to reserve or provision and whether you will approach the limit.

Q64. What is burst concurrency, and how does scaling work beyond it? On a sudden spike Lambda provides an initial burst of environments instantly, then scales at a steady rate (for example +500 environments per function per minute in supported regions) until demand is met or the limit is reached. During ramp-up, requests beyond available concurrency are throttled (sync) or queued (async).

Q68. How do you monitor concurrency across a region? Watch the CloudWatch ConcurrentExecutions metric (account-wide and per function) and UnreservedConcurrentExecutions. Alarm as you approach the account limit, and use ClaimedAccountConcurrency plus the Lambda console and Service Quotas to track headroom.
