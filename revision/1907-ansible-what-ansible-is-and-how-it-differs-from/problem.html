Ansible › 1. Ansible Fundamentals › What Ansible is and how it differs from Puppet and Chef

Q. What is Ansible, and why is it used? Ansible automates configuration management, application deployment and ad-hoc tasks. It is agentless (uses SSH), simple (YAML-based), cuts manual errors and keeps systems consistent.

Q. How does Ansible differ from Puppet or Chef?

  • Ansible: agentless (SSH), push-based, YAML playbooks.
  • Puppet/Chef: agent-based, pull-based, use a DSL or Ruby.

Ansible is simpler and lighter, with no master-agent complexity.
