RAG with LlamaIndex › 4. Introduction to RAG: Pipelines, Prompts, Data Prep › 4.9 Drawbacks of Naive RAG (interview favorite)

Naive RAG workflow: index (chunk and index raw data) → retrieve (search with the query) → generate (query + retrieved context).

StageProblems
IndexingIncomplete or inaccurate extraction; suboptimal chunking; inefficient indexing; poor semantic representation
RetrievalLow precision (retrieved chunks that don't match); low recall (relevant chunks missed); ineffective user queries; redundant or overlapping chunks
GenerationHallucination not grounded in context; hard to judge relevance of retrieved context; over-reliance on context (echoing without synthesis); biased, inconsistent, or irrelevant output

The rest of the course addresses these with advanced and modular RAG.
