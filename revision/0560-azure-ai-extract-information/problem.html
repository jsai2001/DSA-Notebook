Azure AI Services › 4. Language › Extract information

  • Key phrase extraction: "I went to the doctor and got my eye prescription updated" gives doctor, eye prescription.
  • Linked entities: disambiguate by linking to a knowledge base such as Wikipedia ("Empire State Building").
  • NER: identifies person, person type, location, organization, etc.
  • Custom NER: your own domain entities; iterate label, train, evaluate, improve.
  • PII detection: identify, categorize, redact sensitive data (card numbers, names, SSNs).
  • Text Analytics for Health: NER for medical text (diagnosis, medication, symptoms, age).
