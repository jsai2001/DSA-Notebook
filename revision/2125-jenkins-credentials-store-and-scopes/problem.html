Credentials store and scopes Jenkins

Jenkins › 7. Security, Credentials and Access Control › Credentials store and scopes

Q. What is the Credentials provider, and which types can it store? The Credentials plugin stores secrets encrypted in Jenkins and exposes them to jobs by ID, never as plain text. Types: username and password, SSH private key, secret text (API keys, tokens), secret file and certificates. Credentials can be domain-restricted.

Q. What is the difference between credential scopes?

  • Global: usable by any job or pipeline on the instance.
  • System: usable only by Jenkins itself (connecting to agents, email), not exposed to job builds.
  • Item/Folder: scoped to one folder or job (least privilege, good for team isolation).
