Reliability and predictability AZ-900

AZ-900 › 3. Benefits of cloud: availability, scale, reliability, governance › Reliability and predictability

  • Fault = a failure (network, power, hardware). Fault tolerance = you survive a fault and stay available. Mechanisms: availability zones, geo-redundant storage (examples in Sections 5 and 7).
  • Disaster = a large-scale event (hurricane, flood, fire). Plan with BCDR (Business Continuity and Disaster Recovery). Azure Site Recovery replicates VMs to another region, and Azure Backup protects VMs with automated backups.
