Hugging Face Transformers › 3. Text Summarization › ROUGE score

Accuracy and F1 do not work well for summaries. ROUGE (Recall-Oriented Understudy for Gisting Evaluation) measures similarity between the generated summary and the reference text. It is a family of metrics scored from 0.0 (no overlap) to 1.0 (perfect match):

MetricMeasures
ROUGE-1Unigram overlap
ROUGE-2Bigram overlap (phrase-level meaning)
ROUGE-LLongest common subsequence
ROUGE-LsumSentence-level ROUGE-L

Scores near 0.6 indicate a good summary here. Try examples to find the threshold for your use case, then track it on production inference. ROUGE is also used to compare summarization models or configurations.
