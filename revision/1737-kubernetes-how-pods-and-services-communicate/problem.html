How Pods and Services communicate Kubernetes

Kubernetes › 4. Networking › How Pods and Services communicate

Q. How does networking work in Kubernetes? Pods get unique IPs, communicate over a flat network (CNI), and use Services for stable access.

Q. How do Pods communicate with each other? By direct IP or through a Service ClusterIP, enabled by CNI and DNS.

Q. What is a CNI (Container Network Interface), and why is it important? The standard for Pod networking. It assigns IPs and routes traffic (for example Flannel, Calico).

Q. How does Kubernetes handle DNS resolution within a cluster? CoreDNS assigns names like <service>.<namespace>.svc.cluster.local for Pod and Service discovery.
