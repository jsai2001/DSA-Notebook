Kubernetes › 13. Scenario Questions › Releases, migration and recovery

Q. A deployment fails after an update. How do you roll it back? kubectl rollout undo deployment/<name>

Q. How do you migrate an application from a single server to Kubernetes? Containerize it, define a Deployment and Service, move data, and cut over.

Q. A Secret is accidentally exposed. What do you do? Rotate the Secret, delete the old one, audit access, and encrypt etcd.

Q. How do you recover a cluster if the etcd database is corrupted? Restore from backup, restart the control plane, and verify.
