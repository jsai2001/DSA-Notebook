fork, exec and daemons Linux

Linux › 3. Processes and Resource Management › fork, exec and daemons

Q. fork() vs. exec()? fork() clones the process. exec() replaces the process with a new program. They are often used together.

Q. What is a daemon and how is one created? A daemon is a background service (sshd). To create one: fork, let the parent exit, call setsid(), redirect I/O and run a loop.
