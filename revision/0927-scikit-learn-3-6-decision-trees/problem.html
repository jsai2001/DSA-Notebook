Notebook 02_07_decision_tree.ipynb. Ask a question about one feature, split, repeat until a leaf gives the prediction. Splits are chosen to maximize information gain (Gini impurity).

max_depth controls complexity: too shallow means underfitting, too deep means memorizing the training data (overfitting). Splitting also stops early when no further information can be gained.
