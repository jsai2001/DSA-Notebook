Jenkins › 8. Agents and Distributed Builds › Ephemeral agents

Q. Why are dynamic ephemeral agents ideal for the cloud? They are created per build and destroyed after, so there are no long-lived nodes to patch and no leaked state. Benefits: a clean reproducible environment per run, elastic scale (pay only while building) and no snowflake agents. They suit Kubernetes and EC2, where compute is on demand.
