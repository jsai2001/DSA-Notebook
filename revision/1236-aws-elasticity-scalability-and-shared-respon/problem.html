Elasticity, scalability and shared responsibility AWS

AWS › 1. Cloud Fundamentals › Elasticity, scalability and shared responsibility

Q. What is elasticity in AWS? Resources auto-scale to match demand. Auto Scaling adds EC2 instances on a traffic spike and removes them when load is low.

Q. Scalability vs. elasticity?

  • Scalability: adding capacity (manual or planned).
  • Elasticity: automatic, real-time adjustment.

Q. What is the shared responsibility model? AWS secures the infrastructure (data centers, hardware). You secure your setup (data, IAM, configuration).

Q. How does AWS ensure high availability and fault tolerance? Multi-AZ and multi-Region design with redundant systems, for example ELB across AZs and RDS failover.
