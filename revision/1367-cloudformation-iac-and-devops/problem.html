Digital transformation demands faster delivery, but the constraints stay the same.

ConstraintRequirement
AvailabilityApplications cannot go down
SecuritySolutions must be secure
ScalabilityMust handle growth
Multi-geographyDeploy in several regions at once
CostReduce cost and maintenance

Traditional IT is a dangerous road: only specialist ops teams can drive, with long waits and manual provisioning. Modern IT is a freeway: defined rules, guardrails and road signs let more teams move faster. IaC expresses infrastructure as code that deploys in seconds. Governance comes from automatically injected configuration and from monitoring.
