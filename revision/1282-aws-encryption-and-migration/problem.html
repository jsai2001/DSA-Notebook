Q. How do you enable encryption for S3, EBS and EFS?

  • S3: SSE-S3 or KMS through bucket settings.
  • EBS: KMS at creation.
  • EFS: KMS at setup.

Q. How does AWS Snowball help migration? It is a physical device (80 TB) that ships data to S3 for large, slow transfers.
