Depth sweep on training data only (misleading, since the tree can memorize):

Validation curve with a train/test split (the correct way):

  • Train score climbs toward 1.0 as depth grows (memorizing).
  • Test score peaks around depth 10-11, then falls.
  • Left of the peak is underfitting; right of it is overfitting. The peak is the sweet spot.

Here linear regression beats the single tree; trees shine when relationships are non-linear.
