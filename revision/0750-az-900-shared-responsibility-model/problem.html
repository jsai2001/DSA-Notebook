Shared responsibility model AZ-900

AZ-900 › 2. Cloud concepts: models and shared responsibility › Shared responsibility model

  • You and the provider share responsibility. The provider never takes all of it.
  • The provider only covers what it controls. A bug in your app is your problem.
  • The service type you choose (IaaS/PaaS/SaaS) decides how much falls on you. Moving IaaS → PaaS → SaaS shifts responsibility to the provider (see Section 4).
