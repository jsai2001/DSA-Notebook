Q. Define variables at different levels?

  • Inventory: web1 ansible_user=admin
  • Playbook: vars: with app: nginx
  • Role: defaults/main.yml
  • CLI: -e "app=nginx"

Q. Variable precedence? Low to high: role defaults, inventory, playbook vars, CLI -e. The higher one wins.
