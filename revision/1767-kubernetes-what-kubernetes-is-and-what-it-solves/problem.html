Q. What is Kubernetes, and why is it used? Kubernetes (K8s) is an open-source platform for automating deployment, scaling, and management of containerized apps. It is used for scalability, resilience, and portability, handling load balancing, self-healing, and resource optimization across any environment.

Q. What problems does Kubernetes solve in container orchestration? It automates deployment, scaling, load balancing, and self-healing. It manages resources, service discovery, and configs, and coordinates containers across multiple hosts for high availability.

Q. Explain the difference between Docker and Kubernetes. Docker builds and runs containers on one host. Kubernetes orchestrates many containers across a cluster, adding scaling, scheduling, and networking.

Q. How does Kubernetes differ from traditional virtualization? Containers share the host OS, which makes them lighter and faster than VMs that run full guest OSes. Kubernetes optimizes this for scale.
