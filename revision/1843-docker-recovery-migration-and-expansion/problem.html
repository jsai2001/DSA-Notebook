Q. How do you recover a corrupted volume?

Maintain backups, test restored data, and use recovery containers.

Q. How do you migrate a volume to a new host?

Ensure Docker version compatibility, verify permissions, and test after migration.

Q. How do you expand database container storage?

Minimize downtime, verify data integrity, and ensure host disk space.
