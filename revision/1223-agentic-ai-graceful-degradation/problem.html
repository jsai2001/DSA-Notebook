Agentic AI Fundamentals › 13. Robustness and Fault Tolerance › Graceful degradation

Plan for AI to lose functionality gradually: normal operation with full functionality, then degraded mode (reduced but operational), then safe operational mode (minimum functionality, minimum harm). Avoid complete failure (total shutdown). Maintain some functions rather than none.
