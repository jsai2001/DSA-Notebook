Q. What is Amazon EC2 and its features? Virtual servers with flexible instance types, scalable, pay-as-you-go, integrated with S3 and VPC, with Elastic IPs.

Q. EC2 instance types: t2.micro vs. m5.large?

  • t2.micro: 1 vCPU, 1 GB, burstable. Good for small tasks.
  • m5.large: 2 vCPUs, 8 GB, steady performance. Good for web apps.

Q. What are AMIs? Templates (OS plus software) for EC2, stored in S3 and used for consistency and scaling.

Q. How do you secure an EC2 instance? Security Groups, IAM roles, EBS encryption, private subnets, regular updates and monitoring.

Q. How do you monitor EC2 performance? CloudWatch for CPU, memory and I/O metrics plus alarms. Add the SSM Agent and CloudTrail.
