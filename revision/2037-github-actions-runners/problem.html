GitHub Actions › 1. CI/CD and GitHub Actions basics › Runners

Q. What is the role of runners, and what types exist? Runners are VMs or containers that execute workflows. GitHub-hosted runners are ubuntu-latest, windows-latest and macos-latest. Self-hosted runners are your own machines.

Q. What does runs-on do, and what are the common values? It sets the runner type: ubuntu-latest, windows-latest, macos-latest or self-hosted.
