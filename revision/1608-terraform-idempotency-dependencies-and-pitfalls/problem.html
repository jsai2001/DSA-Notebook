Idempotency, dependencies and pitfalls Terraform

Terraform › 7. Best Practices › Idempotency, dependencies and pitfalls

Q. How do you ensure idempotency? Stay declarative, avoid provisioners and use unique IDs.

Q. How do you handle resource dependencies? Implicit dependencies come from references. Explicit ones use depends_on.

Q. What are common Terraform pitfalls? Hardcoding secrets, manual changes, skipping plan, and version mismatches.
