Kubernetes › 12. Advanced Topics › Platform and operations

Q. How does Kubernetes handle multi-tenancy? Namespaces, RBAC, and quotas. Isolation is limited without additional tools.

Q. What is Federation, and when is it used? It manages multiple clusters and is used for multi-region setups or disaster recovery.

Q. How do you upgrade a cluster without downtime? Do rolling upgrades of the control plane and nodes: drain, update, uncordon.

Q. What is the difference between Kubernetes and OpenShift? OpenShift is Kubernetes plus enterprise features (UI, CI/CD, security).

Q. How do you configure a cluster on a cloud provider?

  • EKS: eksctl create.
  • GKE: gcloud container.
  • AKS: az aks.

Q. What is KubeVirt, and how does it integrate VMs with Kubernetes? It runs VMs as Pods, using CRDs and QEMU.

Q. How does Kubernetes handle disaster recovery? Back up etcd, replicate clusters, and use external storage.
