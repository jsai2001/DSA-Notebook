No single perfect option. Choose per workload and proxy between systems:

  • Complex or high-quality: premium Bedrock model (Opus, Sonnet).
  • Simple or high-volume: cheaper Bedrock model (Haiku).
  • Privacy-sensitive, offline or batch: local Ollama.

Evaluate total cost of ownership, not only per-call price. Hybrid cloud + local is the expected norm, like cloud + on-prem.
