Agentic AI Fundamentals › 6. Policy Frameworks › What policy frameworks are

Policy frameworks are guidelines that control and direct agent actions, aligned with values and norms set by architects, designers, developers or end users. They structure how the system makes decisions and follows rules. Without policies, a system could send personal information without consent or perform illegal or unethical tasks.

Decision-making frameworkAligns AI actions with societal, legal and ethical standards
