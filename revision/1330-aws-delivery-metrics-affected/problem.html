Q. Which delivery metrics does blue-green improve?

  • Lead Time for Changes: reduced by 25%.
  • Change Failure Rate: errors eliminated.
  • Mean Time to Restore: minimized through rollback.
  • Availability: preserved by the zero-downtime switch.
