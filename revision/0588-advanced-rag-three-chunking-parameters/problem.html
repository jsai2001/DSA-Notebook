Three chunking parameters Advanced RAG

Advanced RAG › 2. Preprocessing: Chunking, Embeddings, Metadata › Three chunking parameters

ParameterMeaningNotes
Chunk sizeCharacters per chunkDepends on data structure. Start at ~100 words / ~500 chars (a paragraph). May be a hard limit or a guideline depending on the method.
Chunk overlapCharacters repeated between consecutive chunksCarries context across chunks (last sentence of the previous chunk)
Special characters (separators)Characters to split on (periods, newlines)Relax size limits so chunks end at natural boundaries

How separators work: the list is a priority order.

If a chunk exceeds chunk_size without hitting a separator, the splitter relaxes the size limit and continues to the next separator. Result: more coherent chunks, slightly larger.
