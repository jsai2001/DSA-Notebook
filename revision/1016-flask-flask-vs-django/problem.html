Flask vs Django Flask

Flask › 1. What Flask Is and the Project › Flask vs Django

AspectFlaskDjango
TypeMicro frameworkFull-stack framework
SizeMinimal, build only what you needAdmin, auth, ORM out of the box
FlexibilityYou choose structure and componentsConvention over configuration
Learning curveGentlerSteeper, powerful for enterprise
EcosystemSmaller, growingLarger, many reusable apps
  • Use Flask: small/medium apps, flexibility and control, prototypes/MVPs, microservices, charts and dashboards.
  • Use Django: large complex apps, built-in auth/admin/CMS, teams wanting convention, high-traffic apps, strong built-in security.
  • Analogy: Flask is a blank canvas; Django is a paint-by-numbers kit.
