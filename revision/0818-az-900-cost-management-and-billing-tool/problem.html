Cost Management and Billing tool AZ-900

AZ-900 › 9. Cost management › Cost Management and Billing tool

Portal tool for analyzing costs after deployment.

  • Cost analysis: accumulated costs, forecast, breakdown by service/region.
  • Budgets: name, period and threshold.
  • Cost alerts: notify when spending nears or passes a budget (including anomaly alerts).
  • Invoices and payments.
