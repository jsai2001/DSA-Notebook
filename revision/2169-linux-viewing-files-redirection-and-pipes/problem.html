Q. cat vs. more vs. less? cat dumps the full file. more pages down only. less scrolls both ways and is searchable.

Q. How do you redirect output? Explain >, >> and 2>.

Q. What is piping? A pipe (|) sends one command's output to another's input, as in ls | grep txt.
