High availability AZ-900

AZ-900 › 3. Benefits of cloud: availability, scale, reliability, governance › High availability

Availability means the app and its systems are accessible to users. High availability is 99% or higher, achieved with redundant systems.

Threats: network outage, application failure, system (VM) outage, power outage, failure of a system you depend on (such as a database server).
