XGBoostSupervised (both)Boosting: sequential error correction

Key lessons:

  • Always visualize data before and after modeling.
  • Standardize for distance-based and curve-fitting algorithms (K-means, PCA, linear and logistic regression). Don't for trees.
  • Never evaluate on data the model has seen; use a train/test split.
  • Validation curves: sweep a hyperparameter, plot train versus test, and pick the sweet spot.
  • When models score similarly, choose the simpler one.
  • "Is this score good?" depends on the business case; higher is better between models, but sufficiency needs business context.
