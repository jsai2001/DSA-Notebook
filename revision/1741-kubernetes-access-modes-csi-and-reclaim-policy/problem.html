Kubernetes › 5. Storage › Access modes, CSI and reclaim policy

Q. What are the access modes for Persistent Volumes?

  • RWO: read-write, one node.
  • ROX: read-only, many nodes.
  • RWX: read-write, many nodes.

Q. How does Kubernetes handle storage in a multi-node cluster? It uses PV/PVC with node affinity (RWO) or shared systems (RWX) through CSI.

Q. What is the role of CSI (Container Storage Interface)? It standardizes storage drivers and enables dynamic provisioning and advanced features.

Q. What happens to a PV when a PVC is deleted? It depends on the reclaim policy: Retain (keeps the PV), Delete (removes the PV), or Recycle (wipes the data).
