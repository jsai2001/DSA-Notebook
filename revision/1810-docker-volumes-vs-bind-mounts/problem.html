Volumes vs bind mounts Docker

Docker › 5. Docker Storage and Volumes › Volumes vs bind mounts

Q. Volumes vs bind mounts?

  • Volumes: Docker-managed, portable.
  • Binds: a host path, precise.

Q. What happens to a volume when the container is deleted? It stays. Volumes live in /var/lib/docker/volumes.

Q. How do you get persistent storage? Volumes (-v myvol:/data) or binds (-v /host:/data).

Q. --mount vs --volume?

  • --mount: precise, modern.
  • -v: simple, older.
