LinearRegression needs numeric data with no missing values (this dataset is pre-processed).

Prediction = sum of (feature × weight) + intercept. Bar chart of coefficients: larger magnitude means more impact, the sign gives the direction, and near zero means little effect.

Metrics:

MetricMeasuresNotes
R²Variance explained0-1, higher is better
MAEAverage absolute errorNegatives don't cancel positives
MSEAverage squared errorPenalizes large errors more

"What is a good score?" depends on the use case: 0.99 may not suffice for one business and 0.2 may work for another. Between two models on the same data, higher R² wins.
