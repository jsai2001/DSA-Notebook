R² (coefficient of determination) ML Foundations

ML Foundations › 8. Model Evaluation › R² (coefficient of determination)

R²Meaning
≈ 0Explains nothing
0.5-0.8Some signal, maybe enough
≈ 0.9Strong
≈ 1.0Suspicious: likely data leakage (a feature already contains the answer)
  • There is no universal "good enough" threshold. R² is for relative comparison of models on the same data.
  • It doesn't say whether a model is overfit or underfit, or business-ready. Attach a business metric (money saved or lost).
