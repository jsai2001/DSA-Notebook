AZ-900 › 10. Governance and compliance › Azure Blueprints

Reuse configuration, policy and governance for complex deployments. Workflow: Create (define artifacts) → Publish (save to a subscription or management group so it can be assigned) → Assign (resources are created automatically). When assigning to a management group you must name the target subscription, because management groups only hold subscriptions or other management groups.
