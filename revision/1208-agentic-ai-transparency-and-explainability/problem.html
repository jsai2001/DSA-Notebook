Agentic AI Fundamentals › 11. Ethics and Societal Impact › Transparency and explainability

  • Transparency: openly sharing processes, data and the logic behind decisions.
  • Explainability: clearly explaining AI decisions and documenting design and decision-making processes.
  • Benefits: fosters trust and accountability, meets the "trust but verify" process stakeholders want, and creates an audit trail with all actions documented.
