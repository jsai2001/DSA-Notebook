Hugging Face Transformers › 2. Question Answering › QA pipeline

Model: deepset/minilm-uncased-squad2 (BERT-based, trained on SQuAD 2.0). pipeline(...) downloads it to the local cache.

Output fields: score (confidence 0 to 1), start/end (character positions in the context), answer (extracted text).

Gotcha: a low score (0.26 here) signals a weak answer. Use the score as a threshold to filter out low-confidence answers before returning them to users.
