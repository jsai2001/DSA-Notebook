Priority and eviction Kubernetes

Kubernetes › 7. Scheduling and Resources › Priority and eviction

Q. How do you prioritize Pods, and what is a PriorityClass? Use a PriorityClass. Higher priority Pods get scheduled first.

Q. How does Kubernetes handle Pod eviction? It evicts low-priority or over-limit Pods when resources are tight.

Q. How do you troubleshoot a Pod stuck in Pending? Check events (kubectl describe), resources, taints, and PVCs.
