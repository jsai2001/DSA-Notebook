Agentic AI Fundamentals › 4. How Agents Make Decisions › Action sequencing

Define the sequence of actions that leads the agent to its goal state. Security camera crime detection:

  1. Observe the yard.
  2. Detect humans.
  3. Analyze humans.
  4. Determine the humans' intention.
  5. Determine risk.
  6. Determine trade-offs.
  7. Take predefined actions (alert the operator or authorities).
