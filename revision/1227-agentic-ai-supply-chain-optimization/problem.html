Agentic AI Fundamentals › 14. Future of Agentic AI and Technology Integration › Supply chain optimization

Agents predict demand, automate inventory management, streamline logistics and coordinate suppliers using perfect information. Pandemic example: cars and trucks sat in storage waiting for a specific chip, and agentic AI would have obtained that chip from any of many suppliers automatically. Benefits: efficiency, lower cost, customer satisfaction, on-time delivery and better resource use.
