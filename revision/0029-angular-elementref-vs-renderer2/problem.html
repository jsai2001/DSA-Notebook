ElementRef vs Renderer2 Angular

Angular › 9. Directives › ElementRef vs Renderer2

Direct nativeElement access is less safe (XSS risk) and does not work with server-side rendering. Renderer2 is the recommended, safer API.

Other directive patterns:

The direct-style highlight variant (this.el.nativeElement.style.backgroundColor = 'yellow') is the unsafe counterpart of the Renderer2 version.

Testing a directive: mock ElementRef with a spy and inject the directive through TestBed.
