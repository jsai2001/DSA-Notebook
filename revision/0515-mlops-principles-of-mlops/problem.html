MLOps › 2. MLOps Foundations › Principles of MLOps

Goal: an optimal end-to-end workflow that integrates teams, modules and artifacts. Use these as goals when adding any policy, process or tool.

PrincipleMeaning
ModularClear boundaries and ownership; artifacts evolve simultaneously
ContinuousContinuous development, modeling, integration, deployment
AutomatedKey to efficiency and scale
IncrementalQuick time to market via incremental development
End-to-end managedVisibility into the state of all artifacts
ReproducibleArtifact and the code/steps to build it are both managed
ScalableBigger datasets, models, teams, deployments
ObservableMonitoring and diagnosis of workflows and artifacts
TraceableLineage of an artifact back to data and model origins
SecureAccess controls and privacy protection
IntegratedArtifacts move seamlessly across the workflow
ResilientProcess changes don't cascade
TransparentAnyone can understand the inner workings
CollaborativeTeams and artifacts collaborate
OrganizedClear interfaces to outside teams, systems, processes
