Model Context Protocol › 11. Conclusion: Mindset and Responsibility › Security and responsibility framework

Unlimited potential comes with unlimited risk, so limit it at every stage: design, implement, test, deploy, monitor. MCPs should follow three principles:

  1. Do what they are supposed to do: clear purpose and scope.
  2. Do not do what they are not supposed to do: explicit restrictions.
  3. Do not put users at risk: validate and sanitize input, encrypt sensitive data, log security events, require approval for risky actions, give clear errors, never expose credentials.

"When you build MCPs, you are quite literally building the future for other people." Users trust you to protect their data and privacy, act in their interest, be transparent, handle errors, and stay reliable.
