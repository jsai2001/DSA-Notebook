Ansible › 7. Variables, Facts and Vault › Facts and variables

Q. What are Ansible Facts? Host data (OS, IP) collected automatically by the setup module at the start of a play.

Q. Access facts in a Playbook? Use ansible_facts['key'] or dot notation.

Q. Facts vs variables? Facts are dynamic and come from the hosts. Variables are static and user-defined.
