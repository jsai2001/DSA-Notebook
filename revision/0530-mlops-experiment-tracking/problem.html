Experiment tracking MLOps

MLOps › 5. Continuous Training › Experiment tracking

Each training run is an experiment; tracking manages the model's evolution toward the stated goals.

CategoryTrack
Model setupAlgorithm, architecture (deep learning), hyperparameters
InputsDataset and version, train/validation/test splits
OutputsSerialized model (e.g. pickle), metrics (accuracy, errors, F1)
AnalysisComparison with earlier experiments, findings, next steps
  • With config plus inputs tracked, any experiment can be recreated and reproduced.
  • Benefits: measure the impact of parameter or data changes, verify progress toward requirements, decide whether to promote a model, and automate promotion decisions.
  • Manual tracking is slow and error-prone. Use tools integrated into the training pipeline.
