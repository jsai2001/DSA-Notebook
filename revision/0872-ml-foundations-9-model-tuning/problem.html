Hyperparameters are levers set before training that control model complexity. They stay fixed during training (unlike learned parameters).

ProblemCauseSymptom
OverfittingModel too complexMemorizes training data, fails on new data
UnderfittingModel too simpleMisses the signal

Tuning aims for good performance on both train and test.
