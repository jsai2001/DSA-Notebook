StageTeamWhat happensMLOps investment
1. Exploration2–3 engineersStudy ecosystem, explore use cases, build an initial model, pitch strategy to executives. Project may be dropped.None (ad hoc)
2. ExperimentationBigger teamPick one use case, process data, extract features, run training experiments, review against business expectations and cost. Project may be dropped.Incremental: add data/training pipelines as needed; postpone deployment/operations
3. EngineeringFull team (data engineers, scientists, software engineers)Concrete requirements, design workflow, build non-ML software, methodical training/testing/integration, deploy and monitorFull MLOps system (needs people and technology investment)
  • Too early wastes effort if the project is dropped. Too late brings inefficiency and integration pain.
