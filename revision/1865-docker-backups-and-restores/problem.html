Q. How do you back up a Dockerized database?

Use app-specific tools, encrypt backups, and test restoration.

Q. How do you restore a corrupted volume?

Back up regularly, verify integrity, and test the app after the restore.

Q. How do you recover from filesystem corruption?

Back up /var/lib/docker, verify the filesystem, and test containers.
