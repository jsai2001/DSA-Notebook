Azure Portal AZ-900

AZ-900 › 11. Managing and deploying resources › Azure Portal

Web GUI, fully customizable. Favorites pinned in the left menu, a search bar, All Services, docked or flyout menu, themes (including high contrast), and a Home or Dashboard startup view.

Dashboards are custom views with real-time data from Azure Monitor and other services. You can keep several, with tiles you can add, resize and reposition (Edit → drag tiles → Save). They are saved to your subscription and follow you to any device.
