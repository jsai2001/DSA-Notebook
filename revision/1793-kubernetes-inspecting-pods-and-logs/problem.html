Inspecting Pods and logs Kubernetes

Kubernetes › 10. Monitoring and Troubleshooting › Inspecting Pods and logs

Q. How do you check the status of Pods in a cluster? kubectl get pods --all-namespaces -o wide

Q. What is the purpose of kubectl describe? It shows detailed resource information and is useful for debugging events.

Q. How do you view logs of a Pod? kubectl logs <pod-name> -c <container>

Q. What does kubectl get events do? It lists cluster events, which helps identify issues.
