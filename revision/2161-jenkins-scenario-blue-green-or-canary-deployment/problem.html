Q. How do you design a zero-downtime blue/green or canary pipeline? Blue/green: deploy the new version to an idle green environment, run smoke tests, then switch the load balancer or service from blue to green (rollback is switching back). Canary: route a small percentage of traffic to the new version, watch metrics and error rates, then ramp up or roll back. In Jenkins use stages for deploy-green, test, input approval (or an automated metric gate), traffic shift and verify, with automatic rollback on failure.
