PyTorch Hands-On › 5. Broadcasting and the NumPy Bridge › Broadcasting

Lets tensors of different shapes combine without copying data (memory efficient; e.g. applying weights across a batch). Semantics match NumPy. Compare shapes from the last dimension to the first; each pair must satisfy one of:

  • sizes are equal,
  • one of them is 1 (stretched),
  • the dimension is absent in one tensor (treated as 1).

Tensors cannot be empty (at least one dimension).
