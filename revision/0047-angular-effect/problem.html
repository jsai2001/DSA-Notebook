effect() Angular

Angular › 14. Reactive Programming: Signals, effect, async Pipe, RxJS › effect()

Runs a side effect whenever the signals it reads change.

  • Call signals inside the callback to read them.
  • Runs at least once, then again when its signals change. Runs asynchronously during change detection. Should not update the UI or do blocking work.
  • Needs an injection context, so create it in the constructor of a component, service or directive.
  • Use cases: analytics, logging, syncing to storage.
