What Synthetics does AWS CloudWatch

AWS CloudWatch › 12. Synthetics Canaries › What Synthetics does

CloudWatch Synthetics monitors sites, API endpoints and web workflows from the outside by simulating real customer actions, without real customer traffic. It checks that containers, microservices, serverless functions and third-party services work together, and finds problems before customers do.

  • Canaries are configurable scripts that mimic customer actions.
  • They run once or on a schedule, as often as once per minute, 24/7.
  • They check availability and latency of endpoints and the consistency of site content.
  • Alarms on canary metrics send notifications.
