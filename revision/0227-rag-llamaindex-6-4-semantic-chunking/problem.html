6.4 Semantic chunking RAG with LlamaIndex

RAG with LlamaIndex › 6. Advanced RAG: Pre-Retrieval & Indexing › 6.4 Semantic chunking

Instead of fixed N tokens/sentences, use embeddings to find breakpoints where meaning shifts (popularized by Greg Kamradt). Keeps related sentences together; chunk size is dynamic.

How SemanticSplitterNodeParser works: (1) split into sentences (default split_by_sentence_tokenizer, NLTK Punkt) → (2) group adjacent sentences by buffer_size (1 = each sentence alone) → (3) embed each group → (4) cosine similarity between adjacent groups, converted to distance (1 − similarity) → (5) a breakpoint is made where distance exceeds the breakpoint_percentile_threshold percentile → (6) sentences between breakpoints become one node.

ArgumentMeaningDefault
embed_modelModel used for similarityfrom Settings
buffer_sizeSentences grouped together when comparing1
breakpoint_percentile_thresholdPercentile of distances that triggers a split; lower → more nodes95
sentence_splitterPre-splittersplit_by_sentence_tokenizer

Design choices to experiment with: embedding model and its dimensions, buffer size, breakpoint threshold, sentence splitter.
