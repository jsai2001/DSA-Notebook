Azure Service Health AZ-900

AZ-900 › 12. Monitoring tools › Azure Service Health

Reports problems with Azure itself, scoped to the regions where you have resources.

  • Service incidents: unexpected issues.
  • Planned maintenance: upcoming work that may affect you.
  • Health history: past incidents with summary, times and root cause analysis. Download as PDF, share a link, or track on mobile.
