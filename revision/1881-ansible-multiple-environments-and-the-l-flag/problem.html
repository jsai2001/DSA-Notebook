Multiple environments and the -l flag Ansible

Ansible › 3. Inventory › Multiple environments and the -l flag

Q. Inventory for multiple environments (dev, prod)? Use a single file with environment groups, or separate files such as dev_hosts and prod_hosts.

Run: ansible-playbook -i hosts -l dev.

The -l (or --limit) flag limits execution to the hosts matching the pattern, here dev.
