Creating requirements MLOps

MLOps › 3. Requirements & Design › Creating requirements

Requirements cover the whole ML application (ML and non-ML parts together). They keep experimentation from drifting.

Non-ML requirementDescription
User experienceGUIs and APIs
FunctionsData collected, transformations, reporting, analytics
DeploymentHow it is served to customers
ScaleMaximum capacity
SecurityProtection from unauthorized access and damage
ServiceabilityObservability and issue handling
ML-specific requirementDescription
Problem statemente.g. "predict customer propensity"
Performance goalsTarget model performance
Operational goalsHow the model is accessed and used
Cost requirementsCost limits for the model

State ML requirements as minimum metric values the model must achieve and maintain, in two dimensions:

Performance / effectivenessOperational / efficiency
Model metricsAccuracy ≥ 90% in trainingLatency ≤ 1 s (90th percentile)
Product metricsConversion rate ≥ 30%Max 500 concurrent sessions

Good metric goals are measurable (include instrumentation requirements), minimum required, and reasonable. Base them on known performance of existing models in the domain, other market technologies, customer expectations, and what is achievable in the time available.
