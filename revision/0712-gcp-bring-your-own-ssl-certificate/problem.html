Bring your own SSL certificate Google Cloud (GCP)

Google Cloud (GCP) › 3. App Engine: Hello World, Domains, HTTPS › Bring your own SSL certificate

  1. Verify the domain, then disable managed security (Settings, Custom Domains, Disable managed security).
  2. Convert the key to unencrypted PEM and concatenate the certificate chain into one file. END CERTIFICATE and BEGIN CERTIFICATE must be on separate lines.
  3. Confirm that certificate and key match (both commands must print the same hash).
  4. Upload under Settings, SSL Certificates (PEM public certificate + PEM private key), assign it to the domain, save.
