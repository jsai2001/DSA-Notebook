Standardize first, since curve-fitting algorithms need same-scale data.

  • .score() is R² for regression and accuracy (% correct) for classification.
  • A positive coefficient pushes toward the positive class, a negative one away from it, and larger magnitude means more impact.
  • Accuracy trap: if fraud is 0.1% of transactions, predicting "not fraud" always gets 99.9% accuracy and is useless. Tie accuracy to business metrics.
