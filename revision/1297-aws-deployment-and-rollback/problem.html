Q. Blue/green vs. rolling deployment?

  • Blue/green: full switch, no downtime.
  • Rolling: gradual, partial risk.

Q. How do you roll back a failed deployment?

  • CodeDeploy: switch back (blue/green).
  • CloudFormation: revert the stack.
