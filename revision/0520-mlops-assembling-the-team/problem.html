Role% of teamFocus
Data engineers30%Data processing and wrangling
Data scientists20%Building models
Software engineers20%Wrapper services and APIs around the model
ML engineers20%Productizing, scaling, packaging, integrations
Operations engineers10%Deployment and operations
  • Myth: "a couple of data scientists is enough." They are only about 20% of the team.
  • Aim for about 10 engineers for the right balance. With a team of 3, hire people who wear multiple hats.
  • Don't hire everyone upfront. Start with data engineering and data science, add the rest once the model proves viable.
  • Include 1–2 domain experts and 1–2 integration leads.
  • Expect attrition (ML skills are in demand): cross-train and document.
