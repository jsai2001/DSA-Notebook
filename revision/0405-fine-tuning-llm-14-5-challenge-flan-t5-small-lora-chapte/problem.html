Same pipeline with flan-t5-small (76M parameters; structure: embedding → encoder → decoder → LM head). Freezes shared, encoder, decoder and wraps lm_head, counts parameters with TF ops, trains 3 epochs at LR 1e-3 (batch 64 in the lecture variant), then computes BLEU.

Compile/fit/evaluate are as in 14.3; BLEU evaluation is translate_and_evaluate from Section 11 (the notebook's data prep is identical to 14.1).

Results (lecture): total 76M, trainable ~16M (~21%, again the lm_head: 512 × 32,128), training ~3 min (~40 s/epoch after warm-up), BLEU ≈ 0.11 (0.3–0.4 would be very good; larger models score higher), validation loss ~11. Scale up by switching to flan-t5-base/large. For summarisation, evaluate with ROUGE instead of BLEU.
