8. TorchVision PyTorch Hands-On

PyTorch Hands-On › 8. TorchVision

Components: datasets (CIFAR-10/100, ImageNet, Fashion-MNIST, EMNIST, CelebA; detection/segmentation: COCO, Cityscapes, Caltech), io (read/write images as tensors, decode JPEG/PNG from bytes, video via FFmpeg), models (classification: ResNet, EfficientNet, VGG, MobileNet; detection: Faster R-CNN, Mask R-CNN, RetinaNet; segmentation: DeepLab; optionally ImageNet-pretrained, fine-tunable), ops (batched_nms, box_area, clip_boxes_to_image, masks_to_boxes, remove_small_boxes, sigmoid_focal_loss, stochastic_depth), transforms (resize, crop, flip, normalize, color jitter, grayscale, pad, compose; work on PIL and tensors), utils (make_grid, save_image, draw_segmentation_masks).
