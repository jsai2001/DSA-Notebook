Q. A commit triggers 10 parallel jobs and the controller crashes with OOM. How do you prevent and recover? Recover by restarting the controller (temporarily with more -Xmx) and aborting the runaway builds. Prevent it by moving builds to agents (controller executors 0), capping concurrency with throttling, disableConcurrentBuilds and per-job or per-label limits, using a Quiet Period to coalesce rapid pushes, right-sizing executors, adding build discarders and alerting on heap and queue. Ephemeral Kubernetes agents keep load off the controller.
