Stream processing, poison pills and bisecting AWS Lambda

AWS Lambda › 4. Invocation models and event sources › Stream processing, poison pills and bisecting

Q35. How does Lambda handle Kinesis or DynamoDB Streams? Lambda reads records per shard in order, one batch at a time, and checkpoints on success. Concurrency is tied to shards: by default one batch per shard at a time, and ParallelizationFactor (up to 10) allows several concurrent batches per shard while keeping per-partition-key order. A failing batch blocks that shard until it succeeds or is discarded under your error-handling config.

Q38. How does Lambda handle poison pill records in stream processing? A poison pill is a record that always fails and blocks the shard. Mitigations: set MaximumRetryAttempts and MaximumRecordAgeInSeconds so it is eventually discarded, enable BisectBatchOnFunctionError to isolate it, and configure an on-failure destination to capture discarded records instead of losing them.

Q39. What is Bisect on Function Error? BisectBatchOnFunctionError splits a failing batch in half and retries each half, narrowing down until it isolates the single offending record. Good records succeed while only the poison record is retried or discarded, instead of failing the whole batch.
