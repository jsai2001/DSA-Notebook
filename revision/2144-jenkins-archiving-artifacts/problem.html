Jenkins › 10. Artifacts, Fingerprints and Test Reports › Archiving artifacts

Artifacts are objects a job produces that need to be saved, such as compiled binaries (Docker images, ZIP files) or text files (reports). archiveArtifacts is the core build step that identifies and saves them. It usually sits in the post section, which runs after all stages.

Q. How do you archive artifacts, and what is the difference between artifacts and stashes? archiveArtifacts saves files to the controller, viewable and downloadable from the build page for the build's retention. A stash is temporary in-build storage for passing files between stages or agents and is deleted when the build ends. Archive for deliverables, stash for hand-offs within the pipeline.

Q. What is the difference between stash/unstash and archiveArtifacts? stash/unstash move files between stages or agents within one build (ephemeral, not shown in the UI). archiveArtifacts persists files to the controller and exposes them in the UI and API.
