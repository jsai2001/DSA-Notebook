Permission denied for S3 and role trust problems AWS Lambda

AWS Lambda › 13. On-call troubleshooting › Permission denied for S3 and role trust problems

Symptom: "Permission denied" for S3. Add S3 permissions to the execution role (Lambda > Permissions > Execution role > IAM console > inline policy).

Symptom: Lambda cannot assume its execution role. Fix the trust policy so it names the Lambda service (IAM > Roles > role > Trust relationships > Edit).

Symptom: Lambda cannot publish to SNS. Add sns:Publish on the topic to the role.
