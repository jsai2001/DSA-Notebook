2.1 Ethical considerations Fine-Tuning LLMs

Fine-Tuning LLMs › 2. LLM Ethics & Architectures › 2.1 Ethical considerations

  • Bias in training data: LLMs reproduce biases in their data; mitigate with curation and bias detection.
  • Transparency: be able to explain how the model decides.
  • Regulation: guidelines and rules for responsible use.
