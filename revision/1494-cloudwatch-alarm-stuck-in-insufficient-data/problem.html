Alarm stuck in INSUFFICIENT_DATA AWS CloudWatch

AWS CloudWatch › 17. Troubleshooting Alarms and Notifications › Alarm stuck in INSUFFICIENT_DATA

Q. An alarm stays in INSUFFICIENT_DATA. Why? The metric may not exist or has no data points, or the namespace or dimensions do not match.

Console: check the metric in the alarm Details, verify data points under Metrics, and publish test data through the SDK or CLI.
