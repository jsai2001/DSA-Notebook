Data centers AZ-900

AZ-900 › 5. Core architecture: regions, zones, resources › Data centers

A data center is a physical building in a region (at least 2 per region). Each has its own climate control, power supply and generators, and network gear. Cables are owned or leased exclusively by Microsoft. None depends on another's power or water, so one failing does not affect the others.
