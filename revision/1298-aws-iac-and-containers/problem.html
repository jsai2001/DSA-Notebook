AWS › 9. DevOps and CI/CD › IaC and containers

Q. How is CloudFormation used for IaC? Templates define resources. It provisions, updates and rolls back.

Q. What are the benefits of AWS OpsWorks? Managed Chef or Puppet that automates deployment and scaling.

Q. How do Docker and ECS work together on AWS? Containers package apps, ECS runs them and scales them from ECR images.

Q. How do you automate infrastructure provisioning? CloudFormation or Terraform, CLI scripts and Auto Scaling.

Q. How do you handle secrets in CI/CD? Secrets Manager, IAM roles and KMS encryption.
