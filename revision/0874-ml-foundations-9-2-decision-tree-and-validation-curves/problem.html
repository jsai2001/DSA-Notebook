Sklearn naming convention (applies across models):

PrefixRaise the valueLower the value
max_More complex, overfittingSimpler, underfitting
min_Simpler, underfittingMore complex, overfitting
ParameterDefaultRaising it
max_depthNone (unbounded)Deeper, more complex
min_samples_split2Simpler (more rows needed to split)
min_samples_leaf1Simpler (more rows per leaf)
criterionsquared_errorChanges how splits are scored

A validation curve sweeps one hyperparameter using cross-fold validation (each fold takes a turn as the test set) and plots train vs test score.

Reading it: train score keeps rising (memorizing); test score rises, peaks (about depth 8), then drops, so overfitting begins after the peak; a widening gap means growing overfitting.
