Listing and killing processes Linux

Linux › 3. Processes and Resource Management › Listing and killing processes

Q. How do you list running processes? ps aux (all processes), top (real time), htop (user-friendly).

Q. Difference between kill, pkill and killall? kill -9 PID works by PID. pkill -9 name matches a pattern. killall name matches the exact name.

Q. How do you run a process in the background? What does & do? Append & (sleep 100 &) to run it in the background and free the terminal. Use jobs and fg to manage it.

Q. What is a zombie process and how do you identify and remove it? A zombie is a terminated process that its parent has not reaped. Find it with ps aux | grep Z. Remove it by killing the parent (kill -9 parent_PID).
