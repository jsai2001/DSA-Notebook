AWS CloudWatch › 9. Metric Math › Practical tips

  • Use the edit button to replace METRICS() with specific IDs such as M1 so the expression does not aggregate everything selected.
  • Zoom in on the graph, reset zoom for the full range.
  • When several metrics are selected (errors, invocations), point the expression at specific IDs.
