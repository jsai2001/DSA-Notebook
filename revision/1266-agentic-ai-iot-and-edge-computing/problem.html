IoT and edge computing Agentic AI Fundamentals

Agentic AI Fundamentals › 14. Future of Agentic AI and Technology Integration › IoT and edge computing

  • IoT: devices (sensors, thermostats, cameras) collect data autonomously, an agent analyzes it and acts, forming a smart interconnected system.
  • Edge computing: traditionally a device sends data over the network to a master computer, which decides and sends back a response. With edge computing the device runs the agent and decides locally. This reduces latency and bandwidth and keeps working when the network is down.
