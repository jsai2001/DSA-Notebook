Q. What is cloud computing vs. on-premises?

  • Cloud: rent resources online, managed by the provider. Flexible and scalable.
  • On-premises: own and maintain hardware locally. Costly and manual.

Q. What are the key benefits of AWS over others? Global reach, 200+ services, pay-as-you-go pricing, strong security and a big ecosystem.
