RBAC AZ-900

AZ-900 › 8. Identity, access and security › RBAC

Controls what an authenticated identity can do. Three elements:

ElementMeaning
Security principalWho: user, group, service principal, managed identity
Role definitionWhat: permissions grouped in a role (built-in or custom)
ScopeWhere: management group, subscription, resource group or resource

RBAC rules are additive, so a broader role at a higher scope overrides a more restrictive role at a lower scope.

Built-in rolePermissions
OwnerFull access and can assign roles
ContributorFull access, cannot assign roles
