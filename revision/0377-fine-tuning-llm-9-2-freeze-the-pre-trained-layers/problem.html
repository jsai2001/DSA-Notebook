9.2 Freeze the pre-trained layers Fine-Tuning LLMs

Fine-Tuning LLMs › 9. Transfer Learning on FLAN-T5 (en→es translation) › 9.2 Freeze the pre-trained layers

Call model.summary() before and after: ~247.6M total parameters; after freezing about 222.9M non-trainable and 24.7M trainable (~10%): only the output head learns.

  • Freeze the pre-trained layers to keep their knowledge and avoid overfitting.
  • from_logits=True is mandatory for Hugging Face models: they output raw logits (no softmax), so the loss must apply it internally.
  • Counts: Total 247,577,856 (944.43 MB), Trainable 24,674,304 (94.12 MB), Non-trainable 222,903,552 (850.31 MB).
