Availability and scaling AWS

AWS › 8. Architecture and Design › Availability and scaling

Q. How do you build a highly available app on AWS? Multi-AZ ELB, Auto Scaling, RDS Multi-AZ and Route 53.

Q. Horizontal vs. vertical scaling?

  • Horizontal: more instances.
  • Vertical: a bigger instance.

Q. Stateless vs. stateful apps?

  • Stateless: no stored memory (Lambda).
  • Stateful: retains data (RDS).

Q. What is AWS Global Accelerator? It routes users to the nearest edge for low latency and failover.
