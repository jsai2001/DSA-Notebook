LLM capabilities and limits Vector Databases

Vector Databases › 10. Use case 2: RAG concepts › LLM capabilities and limits

  • Capabilities: language (understand, generate, translate, keep conversation flow) and knowledge (answer from training data).
  • As a knowledge source, an LLM:
    1. only knows its training data (no company-specific info);
    2. is outdated (training cut-off date);
    3. hallucinates (confident, plausible, false answers: legal/compliance and trust risk);
    4. has no access to private/enterprise data (internal docs, customer records, specs);
    5. is expensive to update (a custom LLM costs ~$100K-$1M+ to train, plus GPUs, data prep, talent; daily retraining is impractical).
