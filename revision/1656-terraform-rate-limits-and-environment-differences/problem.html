Terraform › 10. Scenario-Based Questions › Rate limits and environment differences

Q. How do you handle a failure caused by rate limits? Lower -parallelism, retry, and split the configuration.

Q. A config works in dev but fails in prod. What do you do? Compare variables, logs and permissions, then standardize.

Q. How do you replicate on-prem infrastructure? Map it to cloud equivalents (VPCs, VMs) and start with a proof of concept.
