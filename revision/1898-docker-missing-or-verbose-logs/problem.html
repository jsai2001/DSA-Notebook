Missing or verbose logs Docker

Docker › 19. On-Call: Logging and Debugging › Missing or verbose logs

Q. No logs from the containerized app?

Configure the json-file driver and verify logs. Ensure the app writes to stdout/stderr and check driver compatibility.

Q. How do you increase log verbosity for crashes?

Enable verbose mode and increase log size. Verify the app supports verbose and monitor storage.

Q. How do you enable debugging for silent failures?

Enable debug and monitor events and logs. Verify debug support and check the config for suppressed logs.

Q. How do you redirect container logs to a file?

Use a volume for log files. Check permissions and monitor log growth.
