Vector Databases › 9. Use case 1: LLM semantic cache › Threshold (L2 distance)

DistanceMeaning
0.1-0.3very similar: hit (0.3 is the recommended start)
0.3-0.5somewhat similar: miss
> 0.5not similar: miss
  • Too low: few hits (strict). Too high: false positives (wrong cached answer returned). Examples from the lesson: "Height of elephant" vs "Weight of elephant" = 0.48, "Capital of France" vs "Population of France" = 0.52 (must miss).
  • Tune on a benchmark set; lesson numbers: 0.1 -> ~20% hit / 98% accuracy; 0.3 -> ~70% / 90% (recommended); 0.5 -> ~95% / 65% (too loose). Score = hit ratio x accuracy.
