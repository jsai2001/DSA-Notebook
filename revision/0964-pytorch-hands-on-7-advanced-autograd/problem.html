PyTorch Hands-On › 7. Advanced Autograd

torch.autograd.grad: returns gradients without touching .grad; finer control, and the base for higher-order derivatives.

Higher-order gradients: create_graph=True keeps the graph so the derivative can itself be differentiated.

Custom autograd Function: define forward and backward yourself (debugging, experiments, unsupported ops). ctx.save_for_backward stashes tensors needed in backward.

Jacobian = matrix of first derivatives (vector-valued functions). Hessian = matrix of second derivatives.

Hooks: register_hook inspects or modifies gradients as they flow during backward.
