AutoGen › 6. Nested Chats (Reflection pattern)

Nested chat = a conversation inside a conversation. An agent consults other agents before replying. Used to model multi-step processes, quality control, hierarchical decisions.

Architecture: User/Reviewer agent talks to the Writer; when the Writer replies, the reviewer runs inner chats with specialist reviewers, then sends consolidated feedback back to the Writer. The inner chats are the "inner monologue" of the reviewer.
