LangGraph › 6. Reflection: Summarizer with Reviewer › 6.1 Design

Reflection: one LLM role generates, another reviews, and the output is refined from the critique. generate → review → regenerate, with an iteration cap.

  • summarizer node: writes the summary, revises it when review comments or user critique are in the history. Entry point.
  • reviewer node: compares original and summary, recommends improvements.
  • Conditional edge after the summarizer: reviewer or END. reviewer → summarizer is a plain edge.
  • Two system prompts define the roles; both can share one model or use different ones.
