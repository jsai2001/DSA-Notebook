Kubernetes › 13. Scenario Questions › Pods, services and nodes

Q. A Pod is stuck in Pending. How do you troubleshoot it? Check resources, taints, and PVCs via kubectl describe.

Q. A service is not accessible externally. What steps would you take? Verify the service type, endpoints, Pods, and network rules.

Q. You notice high CPU usage in your cluster. How do you identify the cause? Use kubectl top, logs, and Prometheus to find the culprits.

Q. How would you handle a node failure? Drain the node, replace it, reschedule Pods, and scale if needed.

Q. A StatefulSet is not creating Pods as expected. What could be the issue? Check PVC binding, resource limits, or a headless Service misconfiguration.
