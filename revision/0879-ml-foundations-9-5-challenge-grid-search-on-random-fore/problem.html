Much slower than the tree: 3 values x 5 folds = 15 fits of 100 trees each.

Why tuning made it worse:

  • Search was incomplete (8, 10, etc. untested).
  • Grid search optimizes on CV folds of the training data, which may not match this test set.
  • Random forest averaging already counters overfit trees, so capping depth can remove useful signal.
  • One parameter isn't enough; try min_samples_split or Hyperopt/Optuna.

Lessons: tuning doesn't always help, grid search finds the best only within your grid, and different models need different tuning (trees benefit hugely from pruning, forests less).
