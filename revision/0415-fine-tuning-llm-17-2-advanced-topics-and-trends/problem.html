Fine-Tuning LLMs › 17. Course Wrap-Up & Where the Field Is Heading › 17.2 Advanced topics and trends

  • Few-shot / zero-shot learning: good performance from minimal examples, better generalisation, lower data barrier (see Section 5).
  • Federated learning: data stays on local devices and only model updates are shared; privacy-preserving, decentralised; used for healthcare, on-device mobile learning and cross-organisation collaboration.
  • Multimodal AI: text + image (visual QA, captioning), text + audio (speech-to-text with context); encode each modality, fuse features, feed one model:
  • Efficient training/deployment: pruning (remove unneeded parameters), quantisation (lower numeric precision), knowledge distillation (teach a smaller model); benefits are lower latency, lower cost, edge deployment.
  • Next topics named in the course: chatbot cloud deployment, model optimisation (distillation, quantisation, pruning), scalable production AI services, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), Constitutional AI, Mixture of Experts.
