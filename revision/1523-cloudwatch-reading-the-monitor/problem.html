Reading the monitor AWS CloudWatch

AWS CloudWatch › 15. Internet Monitor › Reading the monitor

  • Overview: percentage of traffic monitored, availability score, performance score, status active.
  • Traffic health scores: timeline of health events; none means normal.
  • Internet traffic overview: map of traffic origins that shows regions with bottlenecks.
  • Historical explorer: trends over time with performance score, availability score, bytes transferred and round-trip time, filtered by AWS location, country, subdivision, metro, city or network provider.
  • Traffic insights: total traffic in and out, top client locations and optimization suggestions, such as moving an EC2 instance from us-east-1 to ca-central when most traffic comes from that area.
