17.1 What the course covered Fine-Tuning LLMs

Fine-Tuning LLMs › 17. Course Wrap-Up & Where the Field Is Heading › 17.1 What the course covered

  • Architectures: encoder-decoder (seq2seq: translation, summarisation), encoder-only (classification/understanding, BERT-style), decoder-only (generation, GPT-style); match architecture to the task and weigh efficiency against capability.
  • Applications: healthcare (medical text), finance (risk, documents), customer service (support, sentiment).
  • LoRA benefit: 90%+ fewer trainable parameters, feasible on modest hardware.
