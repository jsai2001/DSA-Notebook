Finding bottlenecks Docker

Docker › 18. On-Call: Performance and Monitoring › Finding bottlenecks

Q. High latency in a Dockerized app?

Check CPU, memory, disk and network bottlenecks. Correlate container and host metrics.

Q. A slow containerized app?

Check resources, processes, disk and network. Correlate with logs and identify contention.

Q. How do you analyze high load with Linux tools?

Identify high-CPU and high-memory processes and check trends. Look for Docker processes and monitor historical data.
