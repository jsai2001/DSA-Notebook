Deletion errors AWS CloudFormation

AWS CloudFormation › 10. Errors, Rollback and Troubleshooting › Deletion errors

An S3 bucket that still holds files cannot be deleted, so the stack enters DELETE_FAILED.

Retry optionBehavior
Retain resourcesSkip the failed resource, delete everything else, keep the problem resource
Force deleteDelete everything regardless of errors, like holding the power button; removes the bucket and its contents

Use force delete when you just want the stack gone and do not care about the failing resource.
