Q. Use Ansible with Docker? It needs the Docker SDK.

Q. Integrate Ansible with CI/CD (Jenkins)? Use the Jenkins plugin and credentials.

Q. What are Collections? Bundles of modules and roles, versus standalone roles.

Q. Troubleshoot a failed task? Use -v and --check, add debug tasks, and test connectivity with ping.
