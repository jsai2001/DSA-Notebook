Notebook 03_03_dimensionality_reduction.ipynb. Wide data slows models, overfits and adds noise. PCA finds the directions of greatest spread and builds principal components that keep most information in fewer dimensions. Scale first.

Use PCA when: too many features slow the model, features overlap, you want a 2D/3D visual, or you need to cut noise and overfitting. explained_variance_ratio_ tells how much each component keeps.
