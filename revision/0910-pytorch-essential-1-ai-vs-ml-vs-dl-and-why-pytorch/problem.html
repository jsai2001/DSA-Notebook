PyTorch Essential › 1. AI vs ML vs DL, and Why PyTorch

ConceptDefinition
AISimulating human learning so applications adapt to uncertain/unexpected conditions. Superset.
MLSubset of AI. The computer learns from data to make predictions and improves over time.
DLSubset of ML. Multiple layers of neural networks extract features from raw input.

AI ⊃ ML ⊃ DL. Other AI subsets: expert systems, NLP, robotics.

ML vs DL

AspectMachine LearningDeep Learning
Data dimensionLowHigh
Data volumeSmallLarge
ModelSimplerMany neural-network layers
Feature extractionOften manualProgressive, automatic

Each DL layer takes the previous layer's output, refines it, and reduces error, which is why it is called "deep". Use cases: speech recognition, video anomaly detection, machine translation, speech-to-text.

PyTorch facts: developed by Facebook AI Research (FAIR); Python and C++; first released September 2016; modified BSD licence (free, open source); originally for large-scale image analysis (detection, segmentation, classification).
