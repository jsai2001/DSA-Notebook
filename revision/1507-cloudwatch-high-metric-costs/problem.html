High metric costs AWS CloudWatch

AWS CloudWatch › 20. Troubleshooting Performance and Cost › High metric costs

Q. CloudWatch metric costs are high. How do you cut them? Reduce collection frequency and stop publishing unused metrics.

Find high-frequency metrics under CloudWatch > Metrics > CustomMetrics and update the agent config on the instance. CloudWatch has no delete-metric API; a metric you stop publishing simply expires after its retention window, so stopping the publisher is what removes the cost.
