Provisioners, CI/CD and approval Terraform

Terraform › 8. Advanced Terraform › Provisioners, CI/CD and approval

Q. What is a provisioner, and when should you avoid it? A provisioner runs scripts after creation. Avoid it for non-idempotent tasks and use Ansible instead.

Q. How do you use Terraform with CI/CD? Automate plan and apply, and secure credentials.

Q. What is the difference between apply and -auto-approve? apply prompts for confirmation. -auto-approve skips the prompt and executes automatically.
