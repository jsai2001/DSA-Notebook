What streaming gives you AWS CloudWatch

AWS CloudWatch › 5. Metric Streams › What streaming gives you

A metric stream sends metrics continuously to other services for deeper analytics beyond CloudWatch monitoring. Destinations: Amazon S3 (through Kinesis Data Firehose), Amazon Redshift, HTTP endpoints and third parties such as Logz.io or MongoDB Cloud. You can stream all namespaces or selected ones, and exclude namespaces.
