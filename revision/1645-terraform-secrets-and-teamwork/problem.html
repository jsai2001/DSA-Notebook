Secrets and teamwork Terraform

Terraform › 7. Best Practices › Secrets and teamwork

Q. How do you handle secrets? Use sensitive variables, Vault, or environment variables, never hardcoded values.

Q. How do you manage Terraform in teams? Remote state, workspaces, Git and CI/CD.

Q. What is the role of version control? It tracks changes, enables collaboration and makes code reproducible.
