Read this critically (interview gotcha): inside the loop, layer = LoRALayer(layer) only rebinds a local variable; it does not insert the LoRA layer into the model, so that loop effectively just freezes layers. The Chapter 4 Solution version (small model) skips the loop and applies the same three freezes plus model.layers[3] = LoRALayer(model.get_layer('lm_head')). The reported trainable count after "LoRA" (24,674,304 = 768 × 32,128, i.e. the lm_head size) is identical to the plain freeze in Section 9.2. Always confirm with model.summary() that w_a / w_b appear among trainable weights.

Lecture numbers: trainable 247,577,856 (944 MB) before vs 24,674,304 (94 MB) after, 222,903,552 frozen; GPU RAM ~8 GB vs ~30 GB; 7–10 min for 3 epochs vs 20–30 min full fine-tuning.
