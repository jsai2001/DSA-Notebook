Ansible › 4. Playbooks and YAML › YAML and basic playbook structure

Q. What is YAML, and why does Ansible use it? A human-readable format that relies on indentation. It makes playbooks simple and clear for defining automation.

Q. Structure a basic Playbook?

It targets hosts and lists tasks.

Q. Key Playbook components? hosts (targets), tasks (actions), vars (variables), name (labels), become (sudo), handlers (triggered tasks).
