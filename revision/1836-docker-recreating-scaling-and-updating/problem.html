Q. How do you recreate an accidentally deleted container?

Store configuration in Compose files or scripts, verify volumes, and check environment variables.

Q. How do you scale a Dockerized application?

NGINX config:

The app must be stateless. Monitor resources (docker stats) and use an external load balancer in production.

Q. How do you update a container to the latest application version?

Test in staging, ensure data compatibility, and use health checks.
