  • Colab Enterprise: upload notebooks from local or public Colab; configure resources with runtime templates.
  • User-managed (legacy DLVMs): JupyterLab 3 plus GPU frameworks, customizable OS/networking, SSH to the VM. Full control means full responsibility.
  • Managed: no VM access, built-in CPU/memory/GPU meters, executions and schedules for recurring runs, optional custom container via gcloud.
  • Instances: combine both. Google recommends them and encourages migration off managed and user-managed.
