Most business tabular data problems (databases, spreadsheets) are supervised.

Real-world uses: healthcare (diagnosis, outbreak prediction), finance (fraud detection, market analysis), agriculture (crop yield), transportation (autonomous vehicles), education (personalized learning), business (decisions from tabular data).

Supervised setup used throughout: X = features, y = target label, then split 80/20 (shown in Section 7).
