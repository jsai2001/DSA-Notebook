Agentic AI Fundamentals › 8. Agent Architectures › Planning-centric, GAINs and human-AI collaboration

  • Planning-centric: breaks a complex task (such as 50 steps) into manageable steps, plans and adapts autonomously during execution, and can combine steps for efficiency.
  • GAINs: a form of multi-agent collaboration where agents reach out to others with specialized skills (crime identification, weather prediction) and solve problems together.
  • Human-AI collaboration: AI handles routine tasks, automation and repetitive processes. Humans focus on creativity, problem-solving and higher-level cognitive functions.
