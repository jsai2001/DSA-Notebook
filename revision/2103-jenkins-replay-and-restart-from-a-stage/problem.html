Q. How do you replay or restart a build from a failed stage?

  • Replay re-runs a build with an editable copy of the pipeline script (good for quick experiments).
  • Restart from Stage (declarative pipelines, with preserveStashes) restarts a completed run from a chosen stage, reusing earlier stashed artifacts so earlier successful stages are not repeated.

Both are available from the build page in Blue Ocean or the classic UI.
