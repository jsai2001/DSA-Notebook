10. TorchText PyTorch Hands-On

PyTorch Hands-On › 10. TorchText

Components: datasets (AG_News classification, IMDB sentiment, Multi30K translation, PTB and WikiText-2/103 language modeling), preprocessing (tokenization at word/char/subword level, normalization, padding, truncation, batching), transforms (BasicEnglishNormalize, VocabTransform, Truncate, PadTransform, ToTensor, SentencePieceTokenizer, GPT2BPETokenizer), pretrained embeddings (GloVe 6B/42B/840B, FastText, CharNGram), vocabularies (token <-> index, pretrained embedding integration, subword units). Works with DataLoader, nn.Embedding, and normal train/eval loops.
