ML Foundations › 8. Model Evaluation › Residual plots

Metrics give one number; residual plots show the story (overfitting, non-linearity, heteroscedasticity).

residual = actual - predicted. Positive means the model predicted too low; negative too high.

PropertyGoodBad
BalanceSpread above and below zeroConsistently high or low
VarianceConsistent spreadFanning out at higher values (heteroscedasticity)
LinearityRandom scatterCurve: missed non-linear relationship (add non-linear features for linear regression)
Train vs testSimilar patternsTrain much tighter than test: overfitting

Train vs test residuals together (colored by type) to detect overfitting:

  • CatBoost: train and test spread similar (test slightly wider), healthy generalization.
  • Decision tree: train residuals near zero (memorized), test residuals much wider and fanning. Classic overfitting.
  • Watch y-axis scales when comparing models; ranges differ.
  • Always combine metrics and visualizations.
