Connects spoken text to what the user wants to do. Successor to LUIS (deprecated, absorbed here).

CategoryDescriptionExample
Pattern MatchingStrict, accurate, fast, works offline; no match returns an error"Go to floor nine", IVR "Press 1 for billing"
CLU (Conversational Language Understanding)Natural language; extracts overall intent and entities"What is the stock price of Microsoft?"

CLU: many phrasings ("stock price of", "current price of", "trading at") map to the same intent (get_price; could also be buy, sell) with an entity (MSFT, swappable with GOOG, ORCL). Workflow: define intents with example utterances, build a model, feed it to a recognizer, submit an utterance, read the intent + entity. "What is the stock price of Oracle?" gives intent get_price, entity ORCL.
