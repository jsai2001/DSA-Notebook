CrewAI › 9. Crews and Process

A crew is the central orchestrator: defines the objective (through its tasks), supplies agents and tasks, determines order, runs execution, collects the final result.

ParameterDescription
agentsList of agents
tasksList of tasks. For sequential, list order is execution order.
processProcess.sequential or Process.hierarchical
verboseDefault False. Detailed logs. Recommended during development.
memoryDefault False. Shared memory across agents.
manager_llmLLM for the manager agent. Required for hierarchical only.
ProcessBehavior
Process.sequentialTasks run one after another in list order. Output passes automatically. Best for linear workflows.
Process.hierarchicalA manager agent (LLM) breaks the goal into subtasks and assigns them to agents autonomously. Supports branching and complex paths. Needs manager_llm.
