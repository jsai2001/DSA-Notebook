DynamoDB and Redshift AWS

AWS › 5. Databases › DynamoDB and Redshift

Q. What is DynamoDB and when do you use it over RDS? A NoSQL database that scales fast with low latency. Use it for unstructured, high-traffic data, where RDS is relational.

Q. How does DynamoDB scale and partition? On-demand or provisioned scaling. Partitions are derived from the key and split automatically for load.

Q. What is Amazon Redshift and its use cases? A data warehouse for analytics and BI on large datasets.

Q. SQL vs. NoSQL in AWS?

  • SQL (RDS): structured data, joins.
  • NoSQL (DynamoDB): flexible schema, scales.
