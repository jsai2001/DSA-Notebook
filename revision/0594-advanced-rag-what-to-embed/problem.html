These techniques improve retrieval even when chunks are already good.

Small-to-big: embed a sentence, store the whole paragraph. Use when sentences are very short; retrieving the paragraph adds context and coherence.

Big-to-small: embed the paragraph, store sentences. Use when sentences alone don't make sense (e.g. a period after "Mr." breaks sentence splitting); retrieving sentences separately allows post-processing before the LLM.
