Agentic AI Fundamentals › 5. Machine Learning for Agents › Continuous improvement cycle

Agents with embedded ML are in constant improvement: process new data, refine performance metrics, enhance prediction accuracy, meet challenges with growing robustness, repeat.

  • Security camera example: it adjusts internal cooling from air temperature and humidity, learning from earlier calculations how much to adjust.
  • Human analogy: you go out without a jacket in the cold, learn it was a bad choice, and change your behavior for every later cold day.
