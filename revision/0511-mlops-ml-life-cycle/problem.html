MLOps › 2. MLOps Foundations › ML life cycle

A cyclic process. Retraining is triggered by new training data, new user requirements, or model decay.

  1. Requirements: product owner defines the problem.
  2. Design: workflow for executing the project.
  3. Data acquisition: training data made available.
  4. Feature engineering: cleanse, transform, extract features.
  5. Model training.
  6. Testing & evaluation: refine until performance goals are met.
  7. Deployment & inference: used in business workflows.
  8. Data collection: production data is collected and labeled, which becomes new training data (back to step 3).

MLOps is the ecosystem of processes around this cycle, not the model building itself.
