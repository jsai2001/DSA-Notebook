Vector Databases › 9. Use case 1: LLM semantic cache › Measuring effectiveness

  • Cache hit ratio = hits / total requests; grows as the cache fills. Typical by use case: FAQ 80-90%, customer support 60-80%, general chatbot 40-60%, creative writing 10-30%.
  • Cost example (1000 req/day, $0.002/call, 70% hits): no cache $2.00/day; with cache $0.60/day; saves $1.40/day (~$42/month).
  • Gains are small in the local demo (small prompts, local DB) and bigger in production.
