Variables, backends and HCL Terraform

Terraform › 1. Terraform Basics › Variables, backends and HCL

Q. What is the difference between terraform.tfvars and variables.tf? variables.tf declares variables (type, default). terraform.tfvars sets their values.

Q. How do you define variables and assign values in Terraform? Define:

Assign through the default, a tfvars file, -var, TF_VAR_ environment variables, or the interactive prompt.

Q. Which backends does Terraform support, and why use a remote one? Local, S3, Terraform Cloud and others. A remote backend gives team access, locking and durability.

Q. What is HCL, and how does it compare to JSON in Terraform? HCL is a readable, comment-friendly config language. JSON is verbose and machine-oriented. Both work, but HCL is human-first.
