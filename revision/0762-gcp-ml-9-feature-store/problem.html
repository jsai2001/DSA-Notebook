9. Feature Store GCP for Machine Learning

GCP for Machine Learning › 9. Feature Store

A cloud-native feature management service, tightly integrated with BigQuery. It acts as a metadata layer on top of BigQuery, enabling online serving without duplicating data.

Benefits: centralized feature discovery/access/governance; online (real-time) and offline (historical/batch) serving; native integration with training and prediction.
