Q. A pipeline is stuck on a step indefinitely. How do you debug and safeguard? Open the build's Console or Pipeline Steps to find the stuck step, take a controller thread dump (/threadDump) and check the agent (process, disk, network). Common culprits: a waiting input, a hung sh subprocess, network calls without timeouts, or an offline agent. Safeguard with timeout on risky steps, retry on flaky ones, an overall pipeline timeout option, and a timeout on input.
