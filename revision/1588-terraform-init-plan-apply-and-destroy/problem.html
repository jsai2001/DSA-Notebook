Init, plan, apply and destroy Terraform

Terraform › 1. Terraform Basics › Init, plan, apply and destroy

Q. What is the purpose of terraform init? It sets up the project: downloads providers, configures the backend and fetches modules.

Q. What does terraform plan do, and why is it useful? It shows what will change by comparing desired and current state. It catches errors early and lets you review safely.

Q. How does terraform apply work? It executes the plan: creates, updates or deletes resources and updates state. It prompts for confirmation unless -auto-approve is used.

Q. What is the purpose of terraform destroy? It deletes all managed resources, a cleanup tool.
