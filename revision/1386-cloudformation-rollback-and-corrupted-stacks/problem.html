Rollback and corrupted stacks AWS CloudFormation

AWS CloudFormation › 10. Errors, Rollback and Troubleshooting › Rollback and corrupted stacks

Rollback restores the stack to its state before the failed operation. If the rollback also fails, the stack is corrupted. Recover by deleting and starting over, or by finishing the rollback manually one resource at a time (see section 2).
