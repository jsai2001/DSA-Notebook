Kubernetes › 6. Configuration and Secrets › Securing Secrets

Q. What are the security best practices for managing Secrets? Encrypt at rest, use RBAC, avoid logging, and rotate regularly.

Q. What is the role of RBAC in securing ConfigMaps and Secrets? It restricts who can read or write them via Roles and Bindings.
