Creating data labels MLOps

MLOps › 5. Continuous Training › Creating data labels

Labeling (annotation) adds contextual tags that serve as ML targets. Example: a movie review can carry a sentiment label ("positive") and a rating label (4.5).

Why it matters: unstructured data (text, images, media) often has no labels, available labels may be inaccurate or incomplete, and one dataset may need extra labels for different use cases (sentiment and recommendations).

MethodScaleAccuracyCost
Domain expertsLowHighHigh
CrowdsourcingHighVariable (knowledge/bias)Low
Professional annotatorsMediumHighHigh
Programmatic labelingMassiveDepends on the labeling modelLow (but time to build)

Recommended: combine methods. Label a base dataset with experts or professionals, then use it to train volunteers or programs to label a much larger corpus. Programmatic labeling is a fast-growing MLOps area.
