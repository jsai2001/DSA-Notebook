8.1 Hybrid retrieval (2/3) RAG with LlamaIndex

Hybrid fusion retriever (QueryFusionRetriever) runs three steps: (1) generate or rewrite several queries from the user query, (2) retrieve with each query across an ensemble of retrievers, (3) fuse and rerank.

Reciprocal Rank Fusion (RRF): for each unique node, take 1/rank from every list it appears in, sum them, and reorder by that total. Nodes that rank consistently high across retrievers rise to the top, with no extra model or heavy computation (paper: Cormack et al., SIGIR 2009).

Fusion modeHow scores are combined
reciprocal_rerankRRF; best general choice
relative_scoreMin-max scale each retriever's scores to 0-1, weight per retriever, combine
dist_based_scoreLike relative_score but scales using mean and standard deviation (less sensitive to outliers)
simpleReorder by original scores
