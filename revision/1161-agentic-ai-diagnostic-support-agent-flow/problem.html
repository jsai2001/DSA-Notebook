Agentic AI Fundamentals › 2. Applications and the Healthcare Scenario › Diagnostic support agent flow

Radiology images, lab results and patient histories go in. ML algorithms analyze the data and send real-time diagnostic recommendations to physicians. The doctor makes the final decision. Example: the agent examines an X-ray, finds a bone spur and recommends possible treatments, and the doctor decides.

Overall results: 30% more patients per clinic, fewer cancellations, better use of provider time, fewer diagnostic errors, much lower administrative workload, and staff with more time for patient care. Agents augment humans and do not replace them. Multi-agent systems give each agent a specific persona (role and responsibilities) for accuracy and efficiency.
