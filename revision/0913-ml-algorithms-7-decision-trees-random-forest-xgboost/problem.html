7. Decision Trees, Random Forest, XGBoost ML Algorithms

ML Algorithms › 7. Decision Trees, Random Forest, XGBoost

Decision trees (classification or regression) recursively split data on feature thresholds to minimize error.

Advantages over linear models:

  • No scaling needed: they look at one column at a time.
  • Handle missing values and categoricals, depending on implementation (XGBoost does).
  • Explainable: you can visualize and walk the tree.
  • Capture non-linearity: they can split on the same feature repeatedly (bathtub, S/Z shapes).
