Agentic AI Fundamentals › 10. Game Theory, Negotiation and Conflict Resolution › Negotiation protocols

Negotiation protocols are predefined rules that structure the interaction between agents, outlining steps, actions and the process for reaching consensus.

Flow: Agent A detects a violation and tells the other agents. If they disagree, negotiation starts. If agreement is reached, the conflict is resolved. If not, a human is added for resolution.
