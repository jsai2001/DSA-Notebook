torch.utils.data.DataLoader is an iterable over a dataset. It supports map-style and iterable-style datasets, configurable order, automatic batching, and single- or multi-process loading. Default batch_size is 1. Other parameters: dataset, sampler (custom sampling), num_workers (multi-process loading).

Why mini-batches (e.g. 64 or 128): a full-dataset pass needs lots of memory; mini-batches use less memory and train faster.

shuffle=True for training (random samples), shuffle=False for testing (repeatable results).

Model development steps: design (architecture, initial weights and biases) → training (feed data, compute error, adjust parameters) → validation (unseen data, tune hyperparameters) → testing (final check on fully unseen data).
