Availability zones AZ-900

AZ-900 › 5. Core architecture: regions, zones, resources › Availability zones

Unique physical locations within a region, each with one or more data centers. Zone-enabled regions have at least 3 zones. Not every region supports zones.

TypeWho replicatesExample
ZonalYou choose the zonesVirtual Machines
Zone-redundantAzure replicates automaticallyAzure Storage

Zones give fault tolerance, not disaster recovery. All zones sit in one region, so a regional disaster can take them all. Use region pairs for DR.
