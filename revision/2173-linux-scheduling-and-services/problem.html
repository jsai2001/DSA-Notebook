Scheduling and services Linux

Linux › 3. Processes and Resource Management › Scheduling and services

Q. How do you schedule tasks? cron is for recurring jobs (crontab -e, for example 0 2 * * * script). at is for one-time jobs (at 10pm -f script).

Q. What is systemctl for? It manages systemd services (systemctl start sshd, enable, status).
