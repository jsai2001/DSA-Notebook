5. Metadata Advanced RAG

Advanced RAG › 5. Metadata

Without metadata you are just comparing numbers; metadata is what makes vector DBs useful.

TypeSourceExamplesMain use
Chunking metadataProduced during chunkingSentence number, subtitle, section headerContext + filtering (chunks from one section)
Non-chunking metadataExternal to chunkingAuthor, last-updated date, document titleFiltering (written by you, updated last month)

Storage options: (a) link to an external relational DB, or (b) store directly in the vector store. (b) is more popular for RAG: easier and faster filtering.
