Reading it: depth 1-4 both scores rise, error bars tight. Depth 4 is near the test peak with the tightest error bars (most consistent). Depth 5-6 train higher, test dips then recovers. Depth 9 has very high train score, test drops, error bars very wide (inconsistent across folds). Depths 4 and 6 score similarly, so pick depth 4: when two options are close, choose the simpler one.

max_depth is blunt: it forces all 300+ trees to one depth. grow_policy, min_child_samples, max_leaves give finer control, and combining several hyperparameters can improve further.
