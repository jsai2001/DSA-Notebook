Task: plot the relationship between number of bedrooms and price.

Step 1, basic scatter. Bedrooms are integers, so points stack in columns and density is unreadable:

Step 2, lower alpha (1.0 opaque and hides density; 0.1 moderate overlap; 0.05 heavy; 0.01 dense clusters):

Step 3, yformatter removes scientific notation on the y-axis:

Step 4, jitter spreads integer columns horizontally (jitter=0.8 via .opts()), small size=1 reduces over-plotting, and alpha can go back up to 0.1:

What the improved plot shows: most houses are below $1M (the original looked like many at $4M), high-price points are clear outliers, and 3-4 bedrooms dominate.

TechniqueUse when
