AWS CloudFormation › 11. Advanced Features › Signals

A more powerful form of DependsOn for dependencies on things CloudFormation cannot see. For example, web service A depends on web service B being ready, and CloudFormation cannot know when B is ready. Program service B to send a signal to CloudFormation when it is ready, and CloudFormation then starts creating service A. Signals are rarely used and are very advanced.
