When to customize a pretrained model Hugging Face Transformers

Hugging Face Transformers › 6. Deploying Models in Production › When to customize a pretrained model

  • Low accuracy out of the box.
  • Domain vocabulary or grammar (medical, legal, tweets, regional variants).
  • Reduce size by cutting encoder/decoder layers, then check performance still holds.

Transfer learning guidance: small, specific datasets covering all scenarios equally; accurately labeled, high-quality data; change the architecture one step at a time and check impact; validate across scenarios; monitor production for drift and bias; retrain as new data arrives.
