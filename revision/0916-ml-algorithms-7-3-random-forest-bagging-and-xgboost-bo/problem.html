7.3 Random Forest (bagging) and XGBoost (boosting) ML Algorithms

ML Algorithms › 7. Decision Trees, Random Forest, XGBoost › 7.3 Random Forest (bagging) and XGBoost (boosting)

Single trees overfit; ensembles combine many trees.

Random Forest: train many trees, each on a random subset of columns (and optionally rows). The final answer is the average (regression) or vote (classification). Analogy: you and 40 friends each hit a golf ball once, and the average landing spot is closer to the hole than a typical single hit.

The test score flattens around depth 11-13. When several depths score similarly, pick the shallower model.

XGBoost: train a tree, then train another that corrects the previous tree's errors, and repeat. The prediction is the sum of all trees' contributions. Analogy: hit the golf ball again from where it landed, each swing correcting the last.
