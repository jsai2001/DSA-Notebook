Hyperparameter categories (alternate names in parentheses):

CategoryParametersPurpose
Boostingiterations (num_trees, n_estimators), learning_rate (eta), early_stopping_roundsHow many swings, how hard
Treedepth (max_depth), grow_policy, min_child_samples (min_data_in_leaf), max_leaves (num_leaves)Shape of each tree
Samplingsubsample, sampling_frequency, rsm (colsample_bylevel), random_strength, bagging_temperatureWhat each tree sees
Regularizationl2_leaf_reg (reg_lambda), model_shrink_ratePenalize over-reliance on features
Constraintsmonotone_constraints, feature_weightsForce logical relations (more sqft means higher price); good for explaining to stakeholders

Learning rate: high is like hitting as hard as you can (may overshoot); low is an easy swing (more accurate, needs more iterations). Lower often gives better models. View all parameters with catboost.CatBoostRegressor?.

Early stopping halts training when the eval score hasn't improved for N rounds. It requires an eval_set. iterations=3000 is the cap; here it stopped near iteration 357.

Here CatBoost is used directly (not in a pipeline): cat_features=['zipcode'] lets it handle the categorical natively.
