Scenario: migrating 200 Freestyle jobs to Declarative Pipelines Jenkins

Q. What is the step-by-step strategy?

  1. Inventory and categorize jobs by pattern (build, deploy, cron).
  2. Build templates and Shared Library steps for each common pattern.
  3. Pilot-migrate a few representative jobs to Jenkinsfiles in their repos and run them in parallel with the old jobs to compare.
  4. Roll out by category, converting configs to declarative plus Multibranch.
  5. Standardize credentials through the credentials store and parameterize jobs.
  6. Decommission Freestyle jobs once the pipelines are verified.
  7. Add JCasC so the setup is reproducible.

Migrate incrementally, never big-bang.
