12.3 LoRA (Low-Rank Adaptation): the math Fine-Tuning LLMs

Fine-Tuning LLMs › 12. PEFT & LoRA Concepts › 12.3 LoRA (Low-Rank Adaptation): the math

  • Keep the original weight matrix W frozen (e.g. 512×512 = 262,144 parameters).
  • Learn a low-rank update ΔW = A × B where A is (in × r) and B is (r × out) and r (rank) is tiny. Output = W·x + (x·A·B) (scaled by alpha / r in the PyTorch version).
  • For r = 1: 512×1×2 = 1,024 trainable parameters, ~256x reduction.
  • B is initialised to zeros in the PyTorch version, so training starts exactly at the pre-trained behaviour.
