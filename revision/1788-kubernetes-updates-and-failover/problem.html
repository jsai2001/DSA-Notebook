Updates and failover Kubernetes

Kubernetes › 8. Scaling and Availability › Updates and failover

Q. How does Kubernetes ensure high availability of applications? Through replicas, multi-node scheduling, and self-healing via controllers.

Q. What is a rolling update, and how do you perform one? Gradual Pod replacement: kubectl set image deployment/<name> container=new-image.

Q. What is the difference between a rolling update and a recreate strategy?

  • Rolling: zero downtime, gradual.
  • Recreate: kills all Pods, then restarts them.

Q. How does Kubernetes handle failover for Pods? Controllers (for example a Deployment) reschedule failed Pods to healthy nodes.
