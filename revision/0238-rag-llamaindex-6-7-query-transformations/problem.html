3. HyDE (Hypothetical Document Embeddings): the LLM writes a hypothetical answer; that text is embedded and used for the search. A hypothetical answer is semantically closer to real answers than the question is. It is zero-shot (no labels or fine-tuning) and performs comparably to fine-tuned retrievers. The generated document may contain inaccuracies, but the embedding only has to land in the right neighborhood. Paper: Precise Zero-Shot Dense Retrieval without Relevance Labels.
