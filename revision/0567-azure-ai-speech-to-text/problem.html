ModeDescriptionUse case

Out-of-the-box vs Custom Speech

OptionProsCons
Out-of-the-boxEasy, fast, no training dataWeaker on domain-specific terms
Custom SpeechBetter accuracy for your domainNeeds training data, cost, model maintenance

Custom Speech workflow: collect audio + transcripts, prepare (format, noise reduction, segmentation), create model (acoustic, language, or pronunciation), train (hours to days), test on unseen data against baseline, deploy via APIs/SDKs.

Model expiration: base models are released roughly quarterly (Jan, Apr, Jul, Oct 15th). A model is available for custom training for 1 year and for transcription for 2 years after release.
